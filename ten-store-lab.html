The next workflow

  1. Rehearse the real task with the intended user. Compare with their current method and record failures.
  2. Repair the product and delivery problems the rehearsal reveals. Repeat only the affected checks.
  3. Choose one offer that clears its quality and cost gates. Connect its actual Shopify checkout and delivery.
  4. Run one bounded pilot. Record paid orders, refunds, costs and time before deciding whether to continue.

Calculation and limits

Cash contribution per order = price − included tax − fulfilment cash cost − payment fees − refund/reprint allowance − acquisition cost. Contribution after owner time subtracts minutes × hourly value ÷ 60. Orders to recover fixed costs = ceiling(fixed costs ÷ positive contribution after owner time).

This order model excludes unentered costs, future cash timing, chargebacks and business profit tax. Refund tax reversals and partial-refund timing are not modelled. Use the aggregate ledger for actual transactions. No conversion rate, traffic volume or revenue uplift is invented here.

Checks before a paid launch

Digital tests are evidence for the cases they cover. A paid pilot requires the other parts of the promise to work too.

Before rehearsal

The record can be trusted

Check calculation fixtures, bad-input handling and actual backup recovery. Open the export and print it. Re-run affected paths after a repair.

Stop if a valid backup cannot reopen or an output disagrees with a hand calculation.

Before an offer opens

The intended user finishes the job

Maker, host, coach, grower or crocheter review depends on the product. Museum Key needs unfamiliar players; Fold Street needs assembled paper models.

Stop if the person needs unexplained workarounds or the physical result does not meet the stated scope.

Before accepting payment

The delivery and cost are real

Confirm the product file or service scope, accepted production format, exact costs, payment, refund, policies and customer support. Inspect physical samples.

Stop if the price leaves no room for actual labour and obligations, or the delivery route is untested.

Before more traffic

The first pilot earns continuation

Keep paid orders separate from preview clicks and simulations. Record refunds, fees, supplier costs and owner time against the chosen offer.

Stop at the cash cap. Continue only when the observed result supports the next spend.
