# Clothing without a large launch budget

Checked 29 September 2026 against the linked primary sources. UK selling is the working assumption. Prices are published observations, not configured supplier quotes; recommendations and calculations are labelled below. No accounts, purchases or outreach were made.

**A small POD clothing store can start well below £750.** My recommendation is one recognisable audience, roughly 12–24 designs and two or three sampled garment types. Those are planning choices, not a minimum catalogue size. Additional artwork is cheap to list, but every extra garment introduces another fit, print and delivery promise to check. A matching tote or mug can extend the same collection; unrelated departments would make demand harder to interpret.

| Printify Pop-Up | No setup, listing or monthly fees. UK merchants are eligible. An existing custom domain can be connected; buying that domain is separate. Customers pay Printify, which deducts production and shipping before paying your earnings. Checkout currently uses USD. | Shopify with Printify or Printful | Basic is £25/month on monthly billing, or £19/month billed yearly, before relevant extras. The current trial promotion is temporary. Unlike Pop-Up, supplier charges come from your card/balance separately from the store's payout: allow cash for orders awaiting payout. [Shopify pricing](https://www.shopify.com/uk/pricing), [Printify payment flow](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4483601124113-How-does-the-payment-process-work), [Printful charges](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50261163157905-How-much-does-Printful-cost). |

Pop-Up is the cheapest of these demand tests. It supports [GA4 and Meta Pixel](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/14813624741393-How-can-I-set-up-marketing-tools-for-my-Pop-Up-Store); its [Store Builder documentation](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/42021298071825-How-can-I-customize-my-Pop-Up-Store-using-the-Store-Builder) covers editable text, images, sections and navigation. [Collections](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/44241620713105-How-do-I-create-Product-Collections-with-Pop-Up-Store-Builder) are also documented, although the marketing page still describes some features as forthcoming and calls Builder a limited beta. I found no official documentation establishing merchant-editable HTML, schema, SEO metadata or a native Merchant Center feed. Those capabilities remain unverified. I would choose Shopify when developing software for Shopify merchants is the primary purpose.

Printful has a free account tier and no order minimum. Its UK page quotes adult shirts at **£8.10–£17.94 including one standard print placement**; the UK shirt shipping table shows **£3.69 for the first item**, then £1.20 per additional item. The starting figures calculate to £11.79 before tax, or **£14.15 with 20% UK VAT**, for one shirt. This is an indicative calculation, not a selected SKU quote. Larger sizes, extra placements and other garments can cost more. [Shirt pricing](https://www.printful.com/uk/t-shirt-printing), [shipping](https://www.printful.com/uk/shipping), [UK VAT treatment](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50264510542097-How-is-VAT-charged-on-my-UK-orders). Its advertised 20% sample discount is account-dependent and starts the month after connecting a store; don't assume day-one eligibility. [Sample rules](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50261041946001-How-do-sample-orders-work).

Three coherent clothing directions, proposed as alternatives:

1. **Illustrated everyday basics with unusually clear fit information.** Use a consistent visual style, measured garment dimensions and colour-specific composition. This tests whether an assistant correctly matches fabric and size requirements. For example, the Gildan 5000 has 100% cotton solid colours but polyester blends in several other colours. [Actual blank specification](https://printify.com/app/products/6/gildan/unisex-heavy-cotton-tee).
2. **Personalised reading-club clothing and matching totes.** Names, dates and original club graphics create genuine variants and gift questions. This suits showing products in an existing community, with permission. Printify supports customer text/image input and live previews, within the chosen product's printable areas. [Personalisation](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/34997929259921-How-does-personalization-work-with-the-Printify-Pop-Up-Store).
3. **Pocket-tested patterned leggings for everyday walking.** Compare real waist styles, measurements and phone fit. Printful offers an existing two-pocket garment; fabric composition and weight differ by fulfilment region. This requires more expensive samples and testing than shirts. Artwork does not change the cut, pocket dimensions or fabric technology. [Actual garment](https://www.printful.com/custom/womens/all/all-over-print-recycled-leggings-with-pockets).

Use supplier mockups for accurate print placement, then real samples for fit and detail photographs. AI can improve backgrounds and presentation while preserving the product. It must not invent a better silhouette, pocket, texture or print result. Etsy permits stock mockups for original artwork printed on base products, with stricter first-image rules for buyer-personalised items. [Image rules](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/policy/listing-image-requirements/253962679005). Google requires identifying metadata for generative-AI product images. [Merchant Center guidance](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/14743464?hl=en).

My acquisition proposal is sample-based short videos, design-process posts and participation in one relevant community. Start by filming yourself or a willing wearer. Commission one reusable try-on video only once a sample is satisfactory; obtain a real quote and usage rights. Paying for a video buys content, not distribution. Etsy sellers can inspect real search and listing counts through 15 free keyword searches weekly, but those counts do not prove sales demand. [Marketplace Insights](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/35122361353239-How-Do-I-Use-Etsy-s-Marketplace-Insights-Tool).

Early useful outcomes include buyer questions, observed fit mistakes, correct assistant answers and completed purchases. No formal power threshold is needed to fix an obvious problem. Record acquisition source, paid orders, refunds and fulfilment cost; describe small-sample changes as directional. More cheap listings improve catalogue coverage, but do not establish demand or prove AEO conversion lift.
