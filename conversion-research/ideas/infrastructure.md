# Infrastructure ideas for incremental AI-origin purchases

Research snapshot: 29 September 2026. Ten proposals; full experiment, dependency, pricing-test, and falsification records are in [infrastructure.json](infrastructure.json). Every purchase effect below is a hypothesis. The reviewed sources establish interfaces and existing capabilities, not a measured uplift from these proposed products.

The strongest initial targets are merchants with observable purchase failures after selection. A missing offer may never reach checkout; an approval timeout or lost basket can be tied to a specific attempted purchase. That makes the latter easier to diagnose and test, although a low fault rate can still make a purchase experiment prohibitively large.

Access is a real dependency. OpenAI feed onboarding requires approved-partner access, and its current checkout-plugin contract is an approved-partner beta. Google requires approval before a UCP integration goes live and explicitly distinguishes the full standard from capabilities deployed on its own experiences. A protocol-valid implementation does not establish distribution on either platform. Google also says native checkout does not improve offer ranking. [OpenAI feed onboarding](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/guides/get-started), [OpenAI checkout contract](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/guides/product-checkout-conversion-spec), [Google implementation guide](https://developers.google.com/merchant/ucp/guides/overview), [Google FAQ](https://developers.google.com/merchant/ucp/faq).

The proposed controlled proxy is useful for replaying product responses and screening agent behavior. It cannot turn a synthetic recommendation into a purchase or establish exact reproduction of a consumer engine. For infrastructure work, extend it with deterministic commerce fixtures: stale reads, lost responses, delayed webhooks, expired holds, mismatched units, browser handoffs, and buyer changes. Sandbox completion is a correctness check. Live merchant assignment and actual paid orders establish a purchase effect. Repeated identical simulated shoppers do not add independent customer evidence.

For live trials, freeze the AI-origin classification before assignment. Prefer authenticated platform transactions and known partner sessions; use tagged referrals for the clearly identified referral subset. Do not infer unseen AI Mode purchases from all organic traffic. Count everyone assigned, including buyers who never reach checkout. Follow cancellations and refunds for 30 days, and separate gross paid purchases from retained contribution. Shared inventory, capacity, and public catalog exposure require cluster experiments; ordinary session A/B tests can otherwise hide displacement or contamination.

1. **Recover the exact basket when checkout leaves the agent.**\
   A shopper has already selected the item, but a payment challenge, checkout extension, or unsupported embedded path requires a browser handoff. Build a merchant adapter that records the selected basket, opens the supported destination, reads its state back, and repairs supported omissions. The important comparison is whether the same variant, quantity, promotion, destination, and delivery choice survive. A changed total requires renewed buyer review.

   Basic persistence is already available: UCP defines handoff and Shopify supplies Cart MCP and checkout continuation. The commercial hypothesis is better recovery across a merchant's actual extension/browser combinations. [UCP checkout](https://ucp.dev/specification/shopping/checkout/), [Shopify carts and checkout](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/carts-and-checkout).

   Randomize eligible AI sessions before handoff against the merchant's current native path. The primary endpoint is a nonduplicate paid order within 24 hours per assigned session, over at least four weeks and a sample powered from observed failures. Recovered fields are a diagnostic. Kill the claim if the adapter improves state fidelity without improving retained purchases. This is integration-heavy and depends on real checkout write access.

2. **Prevent an older stock update from replacing the newest offer.**\
   A busy merchant can publish inventory more frequently and still regress to older state. Stripe explicitly describes independent asynchronous imports without completion-order guarantees. Its native delta feeds and live hooks are the baseline, not an absent capability. OpenAI's current product API also supports partial updates and retrieval. [Stripe seller integration](https://docs.stripe.com/agentic-commerce/for-sellers), [OpenAI products API](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/api/products).

   Build one controlled publisher over every merchant writer, with source sequence numbers, coalesced deltas, single-flight scheduling, terminal-job reconciliation, and explicit deletions. Record source truth, destination acceptance, and independently observed live state separately. Never label an accepted upload as proof that ChatGPT now shows it. The most interesting recovery may be stale unavailability after a restock, because a checkout-time stock check cannot repair an item that never gets selected.

   Randomize whole merchants or disjoint stock/substitution pools for eight weeks, with propagation wash-in. Measure paid AI-origin orders per merchant-day and all-channel retained contribution. Fail the idea if freshness improves without additional purchases, or serialization delays useful changes. Readback delays and writers outside the adapter are major risks. Integration-heavy.

3. **Reserve scarce stock only once the buyer starts committing.**\
   An exploratory agent may compare many carts before its user decides. Reserving every candidate can deny stock to other customers; waiting until order creation can lose the last unit during a legitimate review step. The proposal is a timing policy over an existing reservation engine: create no holds during search, acquire an atomic hold at explicit checkout intent, share it through handoff, renew only during a bounded verified review, and release promptly.

   Reservations are established native functionality. commercetools documents expiring cart reservations and distinguishes the reservation guarantee from eventually updated inventory read fields. The edge to test is agent-specific timing, not a new locking primitive. [commercetools inventory overview](https://docs.commercetools.com/api/inventory-overview).

   Randomize entire stock pools or merchants against their current policy for at least six weeks spanning stock pressure. Primary outcome: retained paid orders across the whole pool, with an AI-origin breakdown. An increase among held carts can be misleading if other buyers lose more orders. Kill the claim if displacement cancels the recovery. Integration-heavy; hold hoarding, misleading expiry displays, and repeat renewals are the main failure modes.

4. **Finish an ambiguous order once after a lost response.**\
   A completion response disappears after the merchant or payment processor may have acted. Build a durable coordinator that maps one logical purchase to native idempotency keys, checkout status, payment state, stock commitment, and order receipt. It resolves ambiguity by authoritative reads and provider receipts, then returns the existing result or safely completes remaining work.

   UCP already sets safe completion/recovery rules. A basic retry wrapper would be redundant. The proposed advantage is reconciliation across several merchant systems where the current safe fallback leaves the buyer waiting or sends the case to support. It must never try another payment processor while the original payment is unresolved. [UCP checkout lifecycle](https://ucp.dev/specification/shopping/checkout/), [UCP order reconciliation](https://ucp.dev/specification/shopping/order/).

   Inject response loss and delayed events only in the sandbox. In production, assign eligible sessions before naturally occurring faults and compare nonduplicate paid orders within 24 hours. Expect six weeks or longer if incidents are rare. Stop for duplicate captures; reject the claim if native recovery performs equally well. Integration-heavy, with the cleanest causal target among these proposals when a merchant already has documented ambiguous-order losses.

5. **Keep approval decisions inside the platform deadline.**\
   Stripe documents a four-second deadline for both availability and approval hooks, but their failure behavior differs: availability can fall back to the feed while approval timeout declines payment. A single slow dependency can therefore cause a specific, measurable purchase failure. [Stripe hook behavior](https://docs.stripe.com/agentic-commerce/for-sellers/manage).

   Build a dependency-aware hook runtime: parallel independent reads, keep stock read models current, remove unnecessary optional dependencies, reserve time for final authoritative validation, and use an allowed recoverable response when a decision cannot finish. Keep the same business rules. A fast but stale approval is a failure.

   Randomize actual AI checkout sessions between the existing tuned path and the new path after a shadow comparison proves decision parity. Measure paid orders per assigned checkout, not just response time. Run through peak hours for at least four weeks and to a sample powered by deadline-miss prevalence. Guard against oversells, fraud, and incorrect prices. The claim fails if deadline misses fall without retained-order improvement. Integration-heavy. Existing performance tools and Google's latency logger are strong comparators; the proposed advantage is commerce-specific execution under an external deadline.

6. **Land on the selected variant before JavaScript runs.**\
   An assistant can recommend the right size and pack while its link opens a parent default or another market's price. Build a deployable variant resolver that keeps incoming offer identity, initial HTML, hydrated controls, and the cart aligned. Render the selected variant and currency immediately, using the same resolver for human and agent traffic.

   This is not a new HTML audit. Geoffy already verifies live server-rendered content, and Google recommends initial-HTML product markup. The proposed repair covers the full selected-offer-to-cart path. [Geoffy verification documentation](https://geoffy.ai/docs/faq), [Google product markup guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product-snippet).

   Randomize known AI referral arrivals between current and corrected resolution. Measure paid orders for the intended variant within seven days per assigned arrival, with 30-day returns. This tests post-click conversion only; discovery requires a separate clustered experiment. Kill the idea when mismatches are rare or their correction does not produce retained intended-variant orders. Feasible now for a store whose deployment pipeline is accessible. Incorrect cache keys or hydration that resets selection can recreate the bug.

7. **Turn a failed purchase trace into a tested repair.**\
   Capture the merchant-controlled offer snapshot, protocol version, release identity, checkout transitions, provider receipt, order, and refund outcome under one opaque correlation ID. Turn a failure into a minimal sanitized sandbox reproduction, then deploy a concrete fix through randomized merchant traffic.

   Logging and attribution are already native. Google provides both a UCP logger and purchase analytics, while Peec documents referral analytics and page-level joins. The edge is version-specific reproduction that helps a team ship more purchase-generating repairs. Trace storage alone cannot cause a purchase. [Google event logger](https://developers.google.com/merchant/ucp/guides/tools/bq-storage), [Google purchase analytics](https://developers.google.com/merchant/ucp/guides/tools/merchant-center/analytics), [Peec AI referrals](https://peec.ai/blog/introducing-ai-referrals).

   Compare matched merchant integration teams at equal engineering capacity for eight to twelve weeks. Each proposed repair needs its own randomized paid-order trial. The program endpoint is retained AI-origin orders per assigned merchant-month, adjusted for pre-period purchases. Nested trials explain which repairs contribute; retain negative and uncertain estimates and do not sum overlapping effects. Fail if teams merely produce nicer incident reports. Integration-heavy, especially where a realistic replay requires live inventory timing.

8. **Preserve units, totals, and cart state across protocol adapters.**\
   A syntactically valid conversion can still sell two packs instead of two units, apply a quantity scale twice, or omit a field from a replacement update. Build a typed merchant basket with explicit sale basis, pack identity, exact quantities, currency, tax basis, fulfillment choice, and revision. Check its meaning in both directions against the merchant's authoritative calculator.

   Stripe already integrates several protocols. UCP has precise quantity semantics; Shopify Cart MCP uses replacement updates where other APIs use patches. Generic JSON translation is not the differentiator. Difficult merchandise and version migrations are the proposed focus. [Stripe ACS](https://docs.stripe.com/agentic-commerce/for-sellers), [UCP quantities and units](https://ucp.dev/specification/overview/#quantities-and-units), [Shopify cart semantics](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/carts-and-checkout).

   Use recorded and generated edge cases as release gates, then randomize supported complex-basket sessions to native or checked adapters. Measure correctly priced paid orders for the intended quantity within seven days, including returned wrong-unit orders later. Run at least six weeks or to power. Unsupported representations must lead to a truthful buyer handoff. Frontier: full standard support cannot be assumed on a public consumer channel. Reject if native adapters already preserve every commercially relevant case.

9. **Bind the selected delivery promise to stock and capacity.**\
   A product can exist somewhere in the network yet be unavailable for the buyer's destination or deadline. Build an availability-to-promise service joining location stock, picking capacity, carrier cutoffs, store hours, and service zones. Bind a quote to the exact item, location, fulfillment method, and expiry; reserve capacity only after explicit intent where the merchant actually supports it.

   UCP already transports fulfillment choices. The opportunity is operational continuity between the selected promise and the merchant's capacity commitment, which the schema alone does not supply. Existing OMS promise engines remain the baseline. [UCP fulfillment extension](https://ucp.dev/specification/shopping/extensions/fulfillment/).

   With a cooperating agent, randomize shoppers before feasible-offer selection; use destination/capacity clusters when reservations could interfere. Measure paid orders fulfilled by the buyer's declared deadline per assigned shopper for six to eight weeks, alongside paid orders and total cross-channel contribution. Include users shown no feasible offer. Kill the claim if conservative promises suppress more demand than they recover or consume expensive delivery capacity. Frontier because useful execution needs deep OMS integration and actual channel support.

10. **Admit verified shopping agents without opening the whole store.**\
    A broad bot challenge can stop a buyer-directed agent, while allowing any AI-looking user-agent invites abuse. Build a merchant-owned route policy that uses supported verification and platform credentials, distinguishes catalog reads from checkout writes, and preserves normal transaction authority. Apply expiry, scope, rate limits, and merchant-side replay handling.

    Cloudflare already verifies bots and agents. Its documented signature verification does not itself deduplicate nonces; identity is not purchase authority. The proposed value is a commerce-specific policy that recovers real buyers while maintaining abuse controls. [Cloudflare Web Bot Auth](https://developers.cloudflare.com/bots/reference/bot-verification/web-bot-auth/), [Cloudflare verified bots](https://developers.cloudflare.com/bots/concepts/bot/verified-bots/).

    Validate in shadow mode and merchant-owned sandboxes. Assign real partner shopping sessions before challenge evaluation, or randomize merchants if no pre-challenge session unit exists. Over at least six weeks, measure retained paid orders per assigned session or merchant-day, including initially blocked attempts. Requests and page fetches are diagnostics. Fail the claim if traffic rises without purchases or fraud and origin costs exceed recovered contribution. Integration-heavy; no third-party access controls are bypassed.

The JSON records contain unvalidated monthly test prices in GBP: 500 for the variant resolver; 750 for scoped access; 1,000 for handoff recovery; 1,500 for ordered publication, deadline handling, or trace-to-repair; 2,000 for hold timing, semantic adapters, or delivery promises; and 2,500 for ambiguous-order recovery. These are interview and pilot anchors, not demonstrated willingness to pay. Integration work must be priced separately before a commercial commitment.

No percentage uplift is supportable from the reviewed sources. For each proposal, the first economic target is retained contribution from additional purchases exceeding the service fee, runtime, integration amortization, and harm such as displaced sales or refunds. The experiments explicitly permit a zero or negative result. Start with fault prevalence and a correctly implemented native comparator; if the plausible recoverable purchase pool is too small, move to a different merchant or mechanism.
