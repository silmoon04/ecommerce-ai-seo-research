# Gorgias Shopping Assistant / AI Agent

Research snapshot: 29 September 2026. Commercial benchmark. Full idea specifications, measurement plans, source IDs and illustrative economics are in [gorgias.json](gorgias.json).

Gorgias is a useful benchmark for converting shoppers who have already reached a merchant. Its pre-purchase Shopping Assistant answers product questions and uses product, page, cart and conversation context. Chat can present product cards that lead to a product page or add an item to the cart. This is relevant to external AI shopping when an AI referral reaches the store and encounters an unresolved decision. It does not establish that Gorgias makes a merchant more likely to appear in ChatGPT, Gemini or another consumer assistant. Its newer MCP integration operates the merchant's helpdesk account; it is not evidence of consumer shopping distribution. ([Shopping Assistant](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/shopping-assistant-explained-1216108), [recommendations](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/how-shopping-assistant-recommends-products-4996877), [MCP](https://helpcenter.gorgias.com/en-US/connect-your-ai-assistant-to-the-gorgias-mcp-6310546))

Named deployments support commercial relevance. Pepper reports 19% assisted-conversation conversion and an 18% AOV increase. bareMinerals reports first-month conversion and AOV improvements. Neither public case provides enough allocation, denominator or uncertainty detail to treat the result as an independently established causal effect. These are useful examples of merchants paying attention to pre-purchase assistance, not our expected uplift or evidence about vendor profit. ([Pepper](https://www.gorgias.com/customers-legacy/pepper), [bareMinerals](https://www.gorgias.com/customers/bareminerals))

Gorgias's June 2026 platform research is unusually explicit about attribution limits. It reports 14% conversation-to-order conversion, 47% larger assisted baskets and 1.9% influenced web revenue. Around 80% of attributed revenue rests on a conversation preceding an order within three days; about 18% has a suggested-product-to-order match. Higher-intent shoppers self-select into chat. The report also raises an unquantified refund concern and says its benchmark lacks order-level refund status. These figures cannot support a claim of incremental purchases. ([Vendor research](https://www.gorgias.com/research/ai-shopping-assistants-are-now-officially-influencing-sales))

The published May 28 pricing explainer lists USD 0.90 per AI-resolved interaction on most plans, Starter from USD 1, and USD 1.50 Support + Shopping Assistant overage. Treat these as published unit-price proxies checked on the research date, not a complete quote. Current billing documentation is more specific: a fully AI-resolved ticket can incur both a Helpdesk ticket fee and an automation fee; legacy accounts have exceptions. A purchase model must include both, as well as staffing, discounts and integrations. ([AI pricing](https://www.gorgias.com/blog/ai-agent-pricing), [USD plan context](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing/choose-your-plan), [billing rules](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/how-youre-billed-for-using-gorgias-199385))

The obvious improvements are substantially occupied. Gorgias already offers proactive product questions, search-triggered assistance, several selling styles and automated discounts. Gaia, currently open beta, uses ticket data to improve setup and knowledge. A generic FAQ generator, chatbot, recommendation widget or support-gap dashboard would need to beat those native capabilities. ([AI FAQs](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/ai-faqs-proactively-answer-shopper-questions-on-your-website-4662149), [search assist](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/search-assist-and-ask-anything-input-proactively-start-conversations-with-shoppers-1423468), [Gaia](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/gaia-explained-6646561))

| Evidence layer | What is documented | What remains unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offline testing | Free Playground, draft substitution and existing-ticket re-simulation. | Transfer to consumer AI search, or predictive accuracy for human purchases. Enabled test actions can affect real records. |
| Production monitoring | Conversations, recommendations, handovers and attributed purchases. | External-engine recommendation visibility is a separate task. |

The table draws on the [Playground documentation](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/preview-ai-agent-responses-with-test-conversations-828087), [Convert split tests](https://helpcenter.gorgias.com/en-US/use-ab-split-tests-to-create-effective-campaign-messaging-723376), [executive trial announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/romainlapeyre_folks-using-gorgias-were-running-a-30-activity-7384670213583245312-zx_I), and [metric definitions](https://helpcenter.gorgias.com/en-US/how-metrics-are-calculated-ai-and-automation-5666070). The executive post advertises a 30-day, 50% visitor trial and claims average sales improvement; it does not publish the underlying analysis. Accordingly, the JSON marks randomized experimentation as unclear, not absent.

Current metric definitions also differ from the June research dataset: some use net revenue or exclude refunded orders. Handover treatment varies by metric; orders influenced excludes human handovers while some rate denominators include them. An intervention that deliberately brings in experts therefore needs an independent order ledger covering every assigned shopper. This prevents a dashboard definition from rewarding automation at the expense of purchases. ([Definitions](https://helpcenter.gorgias.com/en-US/how-metrics-are-calculated-ai-and-automation-5666070))

Ten opportunities merit direct comparison with the merchant's existing setup:

1. **Continue the AI shortlist.** Invite a referred shopper to share only their chosen products, constraints and unresolved question. Show the imported brief for correction and continue from that point. This tests whether removing repeated explanation produces additional purchases. Gorgias's onsite context, Alhena's SDK metadata, Constructor's conversation memory and Shopify's long-running carts are existing foundations. The proposed work is buyer-approved context translation between providers. Randomize the invitation before uptake; do not compare importers with everyone else. ([Alhena context](https://alhena.ai/blog/alhena-personalization-stack-sdk-metadata-cdp/), [Shopify carts](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/carts-and-checkout))

2. **Repair the claim that caused the referral.** Let a shopper volunteer an external recommendation, then check its decisive claims against approved compatibility or product evidence. Explain a contradiction and offer a suitable alternative. Native Gorgias already checks answer quality, so another generic grader is weak differentiation. The test concerns an incoming claim and the subsequent purchase decision. Count prevented bad purchases separately; only extra suitable retained purchases satisfy the growth hypothesis. ([Native quality checks](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/how-gorgiass-ai-agent-works-1997817))

3. **Ask one discriminating question.** For a shortlist with two plausible items, ask the lowest-effort question whose answer would change the selection, then stop when further answers would not matter. The mechanism is lower decision effort, not more engagement. Compare with native intent-sensitive dialogue using identical products and offers. Fewer questions alone is insufficient; purchase completion must improve without more mismatch returns. Constructor's existing intent refinement makes it an appropriate comparator. ([Constructor](https://docs.constructor.com/docs/products-ai-shopping-agents-learn-about-ai-shopping-agent))

4. **Verify the offer the shopper qualifies for.** Resolve a loyalty or first-purchase offer through the merchant's authoritative promotion engine and show the applicable cart total before checkout. Gorgias documents limitations for segment-targeted automatic discounts and automatic-discount detection on headless stores. That gives a scoped integration opportunity. Alhena already supports authenticated tools, so test whether earlier verified eligibility removes enough friction to justify the extra identity step. Keep the existing offer constant to separate clarification from discount spending. ([Discount limits](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/how-shopping-assistant-uses-discounts-4996871), [Alhena tools](https://alhena.ai/blog/alhena-personalization-stack-sdk-metadata-cdp/))

5. **Recover an unavailable recommendation without discarding constraints.** Verify region and variant availability across chat, email and SMS, retain the shopper's non-negotiable requirements, and offer only viable substitutes. Gorgias already filters regional unavailability in chat but documents exceptions for other channels or unknown location. The intervention targets those paths. Measure qualified substitute purchases per assigned stockout shopper, including unsuccessful recovery; similar-product clicks are not sales. ([Availability behavior](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/how-shopping-assistant-recommends-products-4996877))

6. **Resolve the actual delivery deadline.** A product recommended for an event or repair must arrive in time. Ask for the need-by date and destination, then compare verified fulfillment options, stock locations or suitable variants. Narvar already supplies delivery predictions, so integrate and test a deadline-based decision rather than recreate prediction. Count purchases that actually arrive on time, and never invent a guarantee or urgency cue. False promises can create orders while destroying customer value. ([Narvar Promise](https://corp.narvar.com/promise))

7. **Construct the minimum complete basket.** For dependent products, identify required components, included components and items the buyer already owns. Build the smallest compatible basket within budget and distinguish requirements from optional additions. Gorgias complements, Constructor category-spanning recommendations and Rebuy upsells already cover general bundling. The opportunity is verified completeness: more successful task purchases with fewer missing adapters or incompatible parts. Randomize against current complementary recommendations and follow returns. ([Rebuy Smart Cart](https://www.rebuyengine.com/product/smart-cart), [Constructor](https://docs.constructor.com/docs/products-ai-shopping-agents-learn-about-ai-shopping-agent))

8. **Stop prompting when the shopper is ready to pay.** After an explicit readiness signal, suppress optional search prompts and upsells, confirm the chosen variant and total, and continue to checkout with help still accessible. The theory is that a shopper who completed discovery elsewhere may be harmed by reopened decisions. Rebuy already tests cart variants, so this should be a small policy experiment. AOV and chat engagement can fall while order count rises; retained contribution must still make economic sense. ([Rebuy testing](https://help.rebuyengine.com/en/articles/10305628-a-b-testing-smart-cart))

9. **Reach the right expert before the decision expires.** Give a specialist the precise unresolved question, evidence and cart, with an honest availability estimate. Gorgias already has confidence-based handover and routing. The additional hypothesis concerns timing and scarce-capacity allocation. Randomize shift blocks or queues, hold staffing constant, and include purchases after human involvement. A gain that simply displaces sales from another queue is not incremental. ([Handover controls](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/customize-how-ai-agent-hands-over-to-your-team-6008591))

10. **Recommend against the reason an item gets returned.** Use merchant-reviewed mismatch reasons to disclose the one relevant product limitation and suggest a better variant or cheaper suitable option. The endpoint is more shoppers completing and keeping an appropriate purchase. Generic advice and ticket coaching already exist. Return reduction alone does not prove conversion improvement; the policy fails if it achieves fewer returns by discouraging equally many suitable purchases. Learn from declared needs and aggregated outcomes, not covert sensitive profiling. ([Gaia coaching](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/gaia-explained-6646561), [bareMinerals case](https://www.gorgias.com/customers/bareminerals))

Every proposal in the JSON includes a buyer, comparator, data requirements, randomization unit, purchase endpoint, guardrails and falsifier. Proposed USD 250-700 monthly prices are unvalidated value-based tests. The scenarios are break-even calculations, not uplift forecasts. For example, at a chosen USD 40 contribution per additional retained order, a USD 400 fee needs ten additional orders before extra operating costs. No source establishes that this effect will occur.

Use the proposed search proxy to replay real failure cases and eliminate broken recommendations before exposing shoppers. Freeze the catalogue, record retrieval and cart responses, and test one intervention at a time. Repeating one synthetic shopper fifty times measures that simulation's variability, not fifty independent buyers. The shared conversation's numerical ladders are invented examples; rank manipulation measures sensitivity to an oracle change and does not establish a deployable ranking improvement.

Then randomize real, identifiable AI-origin arrivals before intervention exposure and count all assigned shoppers. Keep unknown origin and self-reported origin separate from verified referrals; do not infer a user's prompt from the referring domain. Native Shopify already reports channel sessions, orders and conversion, and Alhena already links chat to checkout events, so another attributed-revenue graph is insufficient differentiation. A controlled merchant trial can estimate conversion lift conditional on observed AI traffic. It cannot establish incremental external discovery, replicate proprietary consumer ranking, or eliminate channel substitution by itself. ([Shopify reporting](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/agentic-home), [Alhena events](https://alhena.ai/blog/alhena-cart-checkout-events-sdk/))
