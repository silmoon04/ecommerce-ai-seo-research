Research snapshot: 29 September 2026. The strongest opportunity is to connect specific AI-shopping failures to additional retained purchases while working with the merchant's existing feed stack. DataFeedWatch already covers much of the obvious product: editable rules, title generation, previews, feed checks, product exclusions and title experiments. It also advertises dedicated Perplexity and ChatGPT-shopping feeds. A new product cannot claim that feed publishing or title A/B testing is missing. **What its experiments actually establish**

The detailed title-testing guide describes one title per item ID, alternating A/B titles across products, adding URL tracking and comparing results in Google Analytics. The comparison therefore spans different products. It does not document randomized shoppers or a random assignment procedure. Product ordering could correlate with brand, category or variants. That is a design concern, not proof that every customer experiment is biased. The tool already provides preview and winner publication. [A/B mechanism](https://www.datafeedwatch.com/blog/ab-testing-product-titles), [Analytics workflow](https://help.datafeedwatch.com/article/jxnqqyp0z1-checking-performance-of-product-titles-in-a-b-testing-google-analytics-landing-pages-report).

| Case | Reported design and result | What limits the inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United RV | Two categories followed by wider rollout; article reports 17% and 44% CVR increases. | Before/after comparisons; concurrent randomized control and conversion-action definitions are not disclosed. |
| Adalystic healthcare catalogs | Over 20,000 SKUs, roughly half receiving rewritten titles, for 30 days; a roughly 7,000-SKU catalog for 60 days. Paid and organic Google Shopping were observed. | Claims of meaningful findings lack a reproducible numerical treatment effect, arm-level purchase data and allocation/statistical details. |
| Searchmind | May 2018 versus May 2019: CVR 5.11% to 8.40%; conversions reportedly rose 93.65%. | Historical year-over-year comparison leaves seasonality, advertising and business changes unresolved. |

Visual inspection makes the United RV limitation concrete. The screenshot under the 17% paragraph shows 2.33% to 3.37%, with a displayed 44.31% relative increase, comparing 19-day windows in October/November 2024. The screenshot under the 44% paragraph shows an aggregate 1.42% to 1.66%, or 16.73%, across 13-day windows. The images appear reversed relative to the prose. Their campaign interface resembles Google Ads although the prose calls it Google Analytics. These are vendor-reported observations, not a transferable 17% to 44% uplift range. [Article and embedded screenshots](https://www.datafeedwatch.com/blog/ai-feed-optimization-case-study).

The Adalystic screenshot is expressly captioned as example Analytics results, dated May/June 2023, and displays conversions across all events. It cannot establish purchases in the healthcare engagements. Its useful operational lesson is already implemented: test generated titles, then encode successful patterns into repeatable rules. Its 70-character finding should not become a universal rule: Google's title specification permits 150 characters and discusses the first 70 or fewer being visible; ChatGPT may generate its own display titles. [Case](https://www.datafeedwatch.com/blog/scaling-title-optimization-using-ai), [Google specification](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6324415?hl=en-GB), [ChatGPT shopping](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-shopping-with-chatgpt-search).

Purchase measurement itself is also established adjacent practice. Blue Bungalow used Google Ads cart data with DataFeedWatch's product costs, including purchases of a different SKU from the clicked ad. The opportunity is stronger causal measurement for AI shopping, not discovering that transactions can be joined to feeds. [Blue Bungalow case](https://www.datafeedwatch.com/blog/how-to-increase-revenue-with-data-feed-management-tool).

**Where the proposed simulation belongs**

OpenAI documents custom function tools that let an application return controlled product data. That supports a replay experiment. It does not reproduce consumer ChatGPT retrieval, catalog ingestion, personalization or ranking. Shopping research can use merchant data and public retail information; retailers do not receive private user chats. Any proposed preference handoff requires explicit sharing. [Function calling](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/function-calling), [Shopping research](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12911370-using-shopping-r).

The shared conversation's 12% to 67% ladder and 18.4% to 31.7% example are invented illustrations. Fifty reruns of one shopper measure model variability, not fifty independent human buying decisions. Manually moving a product up a result list measures sensitivity to position; it supplies no method for earning that position in production.

Keep four records separate: controlled replay scores; observed live engine outputs; merchant-controlled treatment assignment and ingestion; and reconciled purchases, cancellations and returns. Use varied, held-out buying intents for replay. Then test deployable changes on actual merchant traffic. Count orders per assigned product-family period when discovery itself can change; conditioning only on resulting clicks can hide lost reach. For onsite interventions, randomize before presentation and use all eligible assigned visitors as the denominator.

ChatGPT's organic product results are distinct from ads, and selection considers more than the feed title. Feed acceptance therefore does not guarantee display. A rise in identifiable AI referrals may also replace purchases from another channel. Preserve total-order and return guardrails before claiming incremental demand. [Shopping selection](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-shopping-with-chatgpt-search), [Discovery/feed requirements](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/file-upload/products).

**Ten conversion-first proposals**

1. **Prove whether the paid-shopping winner transfers.** Add seeded product-family randomization, observed ingestion and order reconciliation above existing DFW exports. Compare the current AI feed with a factual alternative while preserving the paid-shopping winner. The gain must be additional retained AI-origin orders, with total orders guarding against channel substitution. The edge is experimental validity and decision quality beyond the existing title split. [Existing split](https://www.datafeedwatch.com/blog/ab-testing-product-titles).

2. **Resolve exact accessory fit.** For a bounded parts catalog, connect manufacturer evidence to model revisions, required adapters and explicit exclusions. An owned assistant or product page asks for the model and returns a verified purchasable variant. Compare against current search and details using retained compatible purchases. Relational fit checking must outperform the existing extraction and compatibility copy. [Existing specification work](https://www.datafeedwatch.com/blog/scaling-title-optimization-using-ai).

3. **Verify the item through cart handoff.** Preserve explicitly chosen variant, seller, quantity and market in a short-lived record, then compare it with the live cart after redirects. Native variant-specific cart links are the active comparator. The edge is catching semantic changes between selection and checkout; adding another prefilled URL offers little. [Native permalinks](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/checkout/create-cart-permalinks), [Agent checkout](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/carts-and-checkout).

4. **Check the actual delivery deadline.** Combine a declared deadline with destination, warehouse stock, cutoff times and the merchant's real shipping engine. Carry feasible quote inputs into checkout. ShipperHQ already provides product-page dates and costs; our hypothesis is better selection and fewer failed promises when the deadline controls the choice. Measure purchases delivered on time. [Existing calculator](https://shipperhq.com/features/product-page-calculator).

5. **Recover stockouts without losing the buyer's requirements.** Offer a few available alternatives that preserve explicitly supplied must-haves. Explain every tradeoff and require confirmation before relaxing a constraint. Shopify already offers stock-aware related products, so compare directly against that. Count any retained shop purchase once, including visitors who choose no substitute. [Native recommendations](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/storefront-search/search-and-discovery-recommendations).

6. **Calculate the right packs for the requested quantity.** Turn a stated requirement such as 120 units into feasible combinations, with delivered total, unit price and surplus. This extends accurate pack attributes into a deterministic buying tool. Test retained sufficient-quantity purchases against current unit-price information; avoid increasing order counts by splitting one purchase. [Native cart operations](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/carts-and-checkout).

7. **Make safety evidence usable and variant-specific.** Tie reviewed manufacturer details, responsible-party records and warnings to the actual product and an approved readable explanation. Article 19 of the current GPSR establishes relevant online-offer obligations for in-scope goods. Both test arms must already comply; test presentation and applicability, never withholding required warnings. The product supplies evidence, not legal certification. [Current GPSR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988).

8. **Repair observed product-identity collisions.** Trace an actual AI grouping error through registered identity, manufacturer data, pack contents, condition and offer IDs. DFW barcode checks, GS1 verification and OpenAI identity fields already exist. The distinct work is locating an incorrect cross-source association and verifying its repair. Test equal-budget resolution against current operator review and measure retained orders. [GS1](https://support.gs1.org/support/solutions/articles/43000734077-what-is-verified-by-gs1-), [Offer identity](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/file-upload/products).

9. **Allocate refresh capacity by purchase loss.** Prioritize changed offers with measured buyer demand and stale-offer failures under a fixed update budget. Compare against existing schedules and native corrections. API acceptance and consumer display must have separate timestamps. This only matters if a real capacity bottleneck remains; faster sync is already widely available. [Google corrections](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12157888?hl=en), [Partial product updates](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/api/products).

10. **Acquire the missing facts that block purchases.** Rank unanswered, consented buyer questions; obtain authoritative evidence through existing supplier workflows; publish the facts and test the outcome. Akeneo already supplies portals and enrichment. Our proposed advantage is purchase-driven prioritization of genuinely unavailable evidence. Compare equal acquisition budgets and keep unanswered supplier requests in the assigned cohort. [Existing supplier workflow](https://help.akeneo.com/en_US/supplier-data-manager-what-do-i-need-to-get-started/what-is-supplier-data-manager).

These are hypotheses, with complete buyer, experiment, dependency, falsifier and cost fields in [the companion JSON](datafeedwatch.json). Native handoff verification, constrained stockout recovery and pack arithmetic are the most immediately testable. Pre-discovery fit/deadline interventions need a cooperating assistant or destination support; publishing an arbitrary endpoint does not make public engines call it.

No proposal has a supportable expected uplift percentage. The JSON uses explicit fee-only break-even thresholds instead. For example, a USD 200 monthly test fee and an assumed USD 25 contribution per retained order require eight additional retained orders before implementation costs. Actual power depends on traffic, fault prevalence, cluster variance and purchase/return lag. Reject an intervention when an adequately powered test excludes useful gain, or when existing merchant tools solve the same failure more cheaply.
