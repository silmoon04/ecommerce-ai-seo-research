# Profound: convert observations into purchases

Snapshot: 29 September 2026. Commercial benchmark. The companion JSON contains exactly ten ideas, full experiment designs, cost controls, failure tests and a source catalogue. None has a measured purchase-uplift estimate.

Profound is a credible benchmark for enterprise adoption and workflow execution. Its September financing announcement reports USD 180 million raised at a USD 1.8 billion valuation and more than 1,000 enterprise brands. More usefully, Estée Lauder confirms its own portfolio-wide partnership. Neither announcement discloses a contract value, Profound profitability or incremental merchant sales. [Profound announcement](https://www.tryprofound.com/newsroom/profound-raises-usd180m-series-d-at-usd1-8b-valuation-to-build-the-ai-platform-for-marketing-teams), [Estée Lauder announcement](https://investors.elcompanies.com/en/news-and-media/newsroom/press-releases/2026/09-14-2026-173210983)

The strongest ecommerce case found is GR0's unnamed health-and-beauty client: approximately USD 1,000 to over USD 100,000 a month in LLM-attributed revenue within months. The agency combined content, citation-source work and other activity. The case lacks a concurrent control, a disclosed attribution specification and costs. It supports investigating a real commercial channel; it cannot supply an expected uplift for this product. [GR0 case](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/gr0)

The current Brands offer is a free seven-day trial with 50 daily prompts, followed by custom Enterprise pricing. Older dollar-denominated self-serve plans should not be silently treated as the current brand offer. [Pricing](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing)

Profound already automates work through a node-based Agent builder. Its September 25 changelog adds Shopping and GA4 nodes, allowing product observations and revenue reports inside those workflows. Ads Studio already creates and manages paid AI-search activity, with documented private/invite-beta access. A new product needs a stronger distinction than connecting analytics to generated content. [Agents](https://help.tryprofound.com/articles/9762251986-agents-overview), [changelog](https://product.tryprofound.com/changelog), [Ads Studio announcement](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/07/3323259/0/en/profound-launches-ads-studio-helping-marketers-generate-and-optimize-advertising-campaigns-across-ai-search-platforms.html)

| Evidence layer | What is documented | What remains unproven |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offline simulation | No public frozen-product-response replay specification located in the reviewed product/help/developer sources. | Absence from documentation does not establish absence from the product. |

Sources: [Shopping documentation](https://help.tryprofound.com/articles/8862399588-about-shopping), [randomized research](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/does-markdown-increase-ai-bot-traffic), [GA4 integration](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/google-analytics), [developer index](https://docs.tryprofound.com/llms.txt). A historical content-release post puts page A/B testing on a roadmap; that is not evidence of its present availability. [Historical announcement](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/introducing-actions-templates)

The separate behavioral study followed 56 participants through 221 codable tasks. It relates visibility to selected brands and attention, not confirmed payments. Instructions encouraged participants to pick options, repeated tasks came from the same people, and the authors acknowledge optimistic significance from task-level independence assumptions. The finding is useful for choosing hypotheses about comparison and uncertainty. Calling its choices purchases would overstate the endpoint. [Study and methodology](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/the-shortlist-is-the-new-shelf)

The shared conversation's controlled search proxy is technically feasible: an application can define a function and return chosen product records to a model. The proposed figures, including the 12%-67% ladder and 18.4%-31.7% comparison, are illustrations, not measurements. Fifty reruns of one shopper estimate response variation under that setup; they do not create fifty independent people or intents. Moving a product from fourth to second position measures position sensitivity while assuming away the difficult problem of achieving that position.

Public function calling and web search do not document an exact replica of production ChatGPT shopping. Context, memory, candidate retrieval, merchant selection, interfaces and checkout can differ. OpenAI describes selection using user context and provider information, while Profound's recent observational work reports substantial retrieval-source changes. A simulator built around web snippets may therefore miss the mechanism governing feed-based offers. [Function calling](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/function-calling), [web search](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/tools-web-search), [shopping behavior](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-shopping-with-chatgpt-search), [retrieval-change research](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/chatgpt-5.6-shopping-transformation)

Use replay to inspect factual comprehension and reject bad changes. Freeze the candidate set when estimating a conditional response effect, then test retrieval separately. Record distinct shopper tasks, multiple model versions, paired payloads and held-out tasks. A candidate that passes still needs a merchant-controlled live experiment and an order outcome. Public ranking cannot be edited by changing a browser response after the production model has consumed its inputs.

The ten improvements below target different failures.

1. **Calibrate replay against retained-order experiments.** Link each deployable intervention to both its replay result and a randomized merchant result. Then compare replay-guided prioritization with a cheaper defect/frequency rule at equal implementation hours. This is an enabling product: it earns its place only if better selection produces more retained orders. DataFeedWatch already tests feed titles, so generic A/B controls are insufficient differentiation. Test across independent merchant/category portfolios and future models; kill the claim if held-out selection is no better. [Existing title tests](https://www.datafeedwatch.com/blog/ab-testing-product-titles)

2. **Carry consented shopping constraints into the exact cart.** In an owned assistant or approved integration, let a buyer carry chosen size, budget and requirements into a matching cart through an expiring opaque token. Compare the receipt against an identical preloaded cart without it, isolating the extra benefit beyond deep linking. The denominator is every eligible shopper randomized before handoff, including failures. Do not claim access to private ChatGPT conversations. Shopify already supports exact-variant cart links and attribution attributes. [Cart permalinks](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/checkout/create-cart-permalinks)

3. **Build complete compatible carts.** For a home-office or repair task, verify required components against manufacturer specifications and existing equipment. Show the minimum working setup, optional additions, delivered cost and stock. Compare against the merchant's existing complementary-product experience with equal prices and discounts. More items in existing orders is not more purchases; count retained purchasing shoppers and incompatibility returns. Shopify's native bundles and recommendations make a generic accessory recommender a weak proposition. [Bundles](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/bundles/eligibility-and-considerations), [recommendations](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/storefront-search/search-and-discovery-recommendations)

4. **Route unmet brand demand to a seller with stock.** Resolve exact product, region, pack size, authorized seller and warranty conditions, then offer verified alternatives when the original offer cannot fulfill. PriceSpider already performs retailer routing and availability work. The additional hypothesis is that an agent-callable, identity-checked resolver rescues otherwise lost brand purchases. Randomize unresolved-offer shoppers and measure orders across participating retailers. A change in DTC share alone fails the incrementality test. Without retailer order coverage, report bounds or an incomplete experiment. [PriceSpider](https://www.pricespider.com/where-to-buy/)

5. **Qualify a delivery deadline during selection.** Let a shopper voluntarily supply a postcode and required arrival date. Return a timestamped estimate, shipping cost and cutoff, then revalidate at checkout. Compare early qualification with the identical estimate first shown at checkout. Narvar already supplies delivery prediction; the proposed advance is when and how an AI-origin shopper can use it. Retained, on-time orders matter more than attractive estimates, and express-shipping subsidies belong in the cost calculation. [Narvar Promise](https://corp.narvar.com/promise)

6. **Resolve obsolete recommendations to an explicit successor.** Maintain a manufacturer-approved model lineage and explain precisely which features, compatibility or prices changed. Preserve old identities and let the buyer choose remaining stock or a successor. Test against existing truthful discontinued-product pages among AI-origin entrants. Schema.org already provides a successor relation; adding that property does not establish engine uptake. The value must come from a correct replacement decision and more retained orders, with no rise in wrong-model returns. [Successor property](https://schema.org/successorOf)

7. **Provide an evidence-backed fit verdict.** An unfamiliar product gets a short shopper-controlled table: meets, fails or unknown for selected requirements, with checkable evidence and a real downside. Randomize the component against the existing PDP, then compare against a simple merchant-written table if it succeeds. Profound FactCheck already validates claims against source material. The additional value must be a better purchase decision, not another confidence badge. A comprehension gain without retained-order improvement remains a useful diagnostic, not proven commercial uplift. [FactCheck](https://help.tryprofound.com/articles/5793584301-about-factcheck)

8. **Attach the correct helmet-standard evidence to the exact product.** Start narrowly with US adult bicycle helmets and supplier-reviewed documentation. Connect the model and relevant production identity to intended use, certification information and evidence. CPSC describes testing and labeling requirements under 16 CFR Part 1203; this software would not certify products or provide regulatory endorsement. Randomize only the optional evidence display. Every arm keeps required warnings, safety corrections and recall information. Measure retained orders for the declared standard-seeking cohort and stop for any evidence-applicability error. [CPSC guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Business-Education/Business-Guidance/Bicycle-Helmets)

9. **Recover valid checkout sessions after a handoff error.** Build a tested merchant adapter that turns actionable state errors into a clear correction or safe checkout fallback. Preserve session identity and require acceptance of changes. ACP already defines current checkout state, fulfillment and error handling, so the opportunity is reliable implementation and recovery UX. Randomize only safe recovery experiences among remediable errors; count one purchase per original intent. The recoverable-error ceiling must support the economics. Access remains integration-dependent. [ACP checkout](https://www.agenticcommerce.dev/docs/reference/checkout), [partner limits](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/app-guidelines)

10. **Allocate AI ads using incremental orders alongside organic coverage.** Profound already joins paid and organic observations. Extend that workflow with randomized paid-lift evidence, using stock, margin and organic coverage to allocate an equal media budget. Compare policies across sufficiently independent market/category cells; keep organic work fixed. Require both AI-origin order gains and no offsetting loss in total merchant contribution. Relevance scores and attributed ROAS are secondary measures. If the ad platform cannot preserve experiment assignments, the causal claim is unavailable. [Existing Ads Studio scope](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/07/3323259/0/en/profound-launches-ads-studio-helping-marketers-generate-and-optimize-advertising-campaigns-across-ai-search-platforms.html)

Start with an observable failure and sufficient order volume. Post-click features permit shopper randomization before treatment; upstream content/feed changes need fixed product-family or market units because the intervention can change who arrives. Keep instrumentation identical, account for cross-product substitution, and include the return window. Confirmed AI origin is a measured subset; unknown origins stay unknown. Concurrent total-order checks help detect channel shifting.

The JSON gives break-even calculations rather than empirical-looking lift ranges. For example, the chosen GBP 300 monthly price plus GBP 150 amortized setup for constraint continuity requires fifteen additional retained orders at GBP 30 contribution each. Those are assumptions for a sales and experiment design discussion. Actual willingness to pay, fault prevalence and contribution remain unvalidated. Reuse existing mapping, cart, delivery and checkout systems; spend compute on unresolved decisions. Profound already meters Agent credits, so cost transparency itself is also occupied territory. [Catalog mapping](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog/mapping), [Agent credits](https://help.tryprofound.com/articles/9359511602-profound-agents-credits)
