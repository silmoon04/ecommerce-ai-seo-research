# TinySEO: ten purchase-focused extensions

Snapshot: 29 September 2026. Commercial benchmark. This review covers public documentation, merchant testimony and vendor case studies; it does not include an installation or a production experiment. The companion [tinyseo.json](tinyseo.json) contains the full ten-idea schema, experimental definitions, economics, dependencies and source register.

TinySEO earns a place in this comparison because merchants already use it for recurring storefront maintenance. Its Shopify listing shows Built for Shopify and a 5.0 rating from 2,520 reviews. Those observations establish distribution and merchant feedback, without establishing paying customers, retention, vendor profit or incremental AI-origin orders. [Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/smart-image-optimizer)

The app combines image compression, metadata and alt editing, structured data, redirects, script controls, lazy loading, preloading and AI content tools. On this date, published monthly prices were US$14, US$23 and US$49, with 1,500, 5,000 and 15,000 image optimizations respectively. Free installation includes 50 monthly images, then US$0.03 each. The website also lists a yearly option equivalent to US$8/month. Feature placement differs between pricing summaries and comparison tables, so precise entitlements need confirmation. [Features](https://tinyseo.com/services/), [pricing](https://tinyseo.com/pricing/)

The economic challenge is clear: another tool must create enough additional purchase value to justify a separate fee above an inexpensive maintenance suite. Merely generating more alt text or offering a speed score is a weak distinction.

## What the evidence establishes

The Caravan Cover Shop case reports homepage First Contentful Paint changing from 3.4 to 0.9 seconds and a tested page becoming interactive in 2.7 seconds after previously taking almost nine. It is an older vendor before/after case, without a randomized order endpoint. [Caravan case](https://tinyseo.com/success-stories/caravan-cover-shop-image-optimization/)

AMARRA's case reports roughly 2.26GB saved and a desktop performance score changing from 48 to 82. That is a 34-point score change. It supplies no basis for estimating a conversion percentage for this product. [AMARRA case](https://tinyseo.com/success-stories/amarra-road-to-customer-loyalty-with-tinyimg/)

There is adjacent evidence that performance can affect purchases: Google's published Rakuten 24 case describes a month-long 50/50 landing-page experiment with reported business improvements. The intervention, store, traffic and period differ from TinySEO and AI shopping. Its result supports testing the mechanism; importing its percentage would be unjustified. [Rakuten experiment](https://web.dev/case-studies/rakuten)

The strongest preservation example is the four-star BE Artistic Designs review dated 28 July 2026. The merchant says authored alt text was overwritten and support restored it. TinyIMG responded that onboarding/pricing warnings existed and their visibility could improve. This is one documented incident with a successful remedy, without a measured sales loss. Restoration is already a product capability. [Merchant review and response](https://apps.shopify.com/smart-image-optimizer/reviews?ratings%5B%5D=4), [restoration documentation](https://help.tinyseo.com/en/articles/3092515-what-if-i-want-to-get-my-original-images-back)

Public TinySEO analytics documentation covers Search Console metrics. I found no documented controlled shopper replay, randomized merchant experiment or AI-origin purchase measurement in the reviewed sources. Isolated live AI-answer monitoring remains unclear. Absence from public sources does not establish product absence. Google includes AI-feature traffic in Search Console's Web reporting and says special AI files are unnecessary for inclusion. [TinySEO analytics](https://tinyseo.com/services/analytics/), [Google guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

## Ten interventions worth testing

1. **Schedule scripts around a functioning purchase path.** For stores with slow AI-referred landings, defer nonessential scripts only after verifying that variant selection, stock checks and add-to-cart remain usable. TinySEO already has script toggles and loading controls. The proposed advantage is choosing changes using working purchase tasks and randomized orders. Persistently assign visitors to existing or validated scheduling; measure seven-day paid purchases and cart errors. Reject a policy that improves timings while damaging buying functions. Unvalidated test price: US$99/month. [Script panel](https://tinyseo.com/services/third-party-scripts/), [lazy loading](https://tinyseo.com/services/lazy-loading/)

2. **Compress against product-detail recognition.** Jewelry markings, fabric texture and connector shapes can be small but decisive. Mark those regions with merchant input, reject derivatives that destroy them, and offer original-detail zoom. Cloudinary already uses perceptual quality optimization, so generic quality preservation is established competition. The new hypothesis concerns product-specific recognition. Randomize existing versus constrained derivatives; measure purchases, recognition errors and subsequent misdescription returns. This needs media delivery integration and may fail when larger files erase the benefit. Unvalidated price: US$149/month. [Cloudinary optimization](https://cloudinary.com/documentation/image_optimization), [TinySEO compression](https://tinyseo.com/services/image-optimization/)

3. **Verify the variant from citation to cart.** Test whether a real referred variant URL selects the corresponding image, price and cart line. Repair faults without overriding the buyer's later choices. Shopify already supports this behavior; the product would find failures across theme and app boundaries. Randomize eligible explicit-variant arrivals and measure seven-day purchasers plus wrong-variant returns. Qualify merchants by observed mismatch volume before selling the repair. Unvalidated price: US$79/month. [Shopify variant guidance](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/product-merchandising/variants)

4. **Make the image-to-cart journey accessible.** Audit meaningful alternatives, swatch names, readable measurements, selected states and keyboard operation through the cart. W3C guidance distinguishes an image's purpose from its literal appearance. Fix known blocking defects for everyone, then test optional explanations between two reviewed accessible versions. Purchase outcomes and consenting user task tests answer different questions. EU e-commerce accessibility requirements have scope qualifications and exemptions; this would provide evidence for review, not legal certification. Unvalidated price: US$149/month, with human testing scoped separately. [W3C images](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/informative/), [functional images](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/functional/), [EU summary](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/EN/legal-content/summary/accessibility-of-products-and-services.html)

5. **Protect authored work while making useful fixes.** Add field ownership, semantic diffs and version-checked writes. Retain approved wording while proposing explicit updates to stale protected facts. TinySEO restoration, Rewind backups and Burnish approval/rollback already cover adjacent needs. The hypothesis is that safe concurrent merging helps merchants deploy purchase-relevant fixes sooner. Randomize reviewed workflows at equal labor budgets; measure AI-origin orders by assigned product-week and restoration incidents. Time saved alone does not establish conversion value. Unvalidated price: US$49/month. [Rewind coverage](https://help.rewind.com/hc/en-us/articles/27722238895003-What-does-Rewind-backup-for-Shopify), [Burnish workflow](https://useburnish.com/what-it-does/)

6. **Show evidence for the shopper's stated constraint.** Index existing verified media against questions such as folded dimensions or connector type. After explicit shopper input or consented partner context, open the relevant image and caption while preserving the full gallery. Do not generate an imagined product detail. Randomize eligible shoppers to this view or the ordinary gallery; measure purchases and returns. This ambitious direction depends on reliable evidence mapping and abstention when proof is missing. Unvalidated price: US$99/month. [Contextual image guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/informative/), [existing content experimentation](https://docs.intelligems.io/general-features/targeting/page-targeting)

7. **Recover purchases from obsolete AI citations.** For retired products, show a truthful discontinuation page with verified successors and explicit differences. Use redirects for genuine moves or equivalents. TinySEO already manages broken links; the additional work preserves the requirement behind a stale recommendation. Randomize stale-link arrivals between the current truthful recovery experience and the successor page, leaving upstream indexing unchanged during the test. Measure purchases per assigned arrival and return complaints. Without meaningful stale demand or suitable replacements, stop. Unvalidated price: US$79/month. [TinySEO redirects](https://tinyseo.com/services/broken-link-fixing/)

8. **Repair image discovery where the storefront actually fails.** Compare raw HTML, rendered content, asset responses and product identity. Repair missing image fallbacks or inaccessible assets where faults are observed. TinySEO documents image-access limits; Geoffy already verifies server HTML, so this cannot be sold as a novel page check. Randomize matched product-family repair rollouts and measure orders per assigned product-week after realistic crawl lag. Conversion among arriving visitors would miss the acquisition effect. Unvalidated price: US$99/month plus scoped repairs. [Image access](https://help.tinyseo.com/en/articles/9106213-what-images-does-tinyseo-optimize), [Google image guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images), [Geoffy verification](https://geoffy.ai/docs/faq)

9. **Allocate repair work by purchases per hour.** Join fault exposure, repair cost and randomized outcomes to rank a constrained repair backlog. Keep uncertainty and an exploration budget visible. TinySEO audits and Intelligems experiments already supply parts of this workflow. The proposed value comes from deploying better work with the same staff time. Randomize comparable agency teams or store portfolios, use equal budgets, and evaluate paid orders at the assignment level. Stop if the queue merely changes dashboard rankings. Unvalidated portfolio price: US$199/month. [TinySEO analytics](https://tinyseo.com/services/analytics/), [Intelligems capabilities](https://docs.intelligems.io/getting-started/general-faqs)

10. **Recover explicitly saved selections across browser handoffs.** If an audit finds material context loss when leaving an AI webview, offer an expiring continuation link after variant selection. Restore selected items, revalidate stock and price, and require acceptance of changes. Shopify already provides cart permalinks, Cart MCP and continuation URLs. The opportunity is repair of observed failures using those capabilities. Randomize the offer before adoption and include people who decline in the denominator. Do not fingerprint users or put personal information in links. Unvalidated price: US$149/month. [Cart permalinks](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/checkout/create-cart-permalinks), [agent carts](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/carts-and-checkout)

## Measurement and economics

Use offline replay to test whether a controlled field, image or tool response changes an agent's interpretation. Freeze the candidate set, vary one factor, and diversify intents. Repeated samples from one shopper prompt measure model variability, not independent human demand. Artificially moving a product to rank two measures sensitivity to rank; it does not prove that a merchant can obtain that position.

Live engine observations test transfer under uncontrolled retrieval. Merchant-side randomization tests purchases. Keep those records separate. The shared conversation's 12%-67% ladder and 18.4%-31.7% figures are illustrations, with no measurement supporting them.

For post-arrival tests, enroll eligible AI-attributed visitors before treatment and preserve assignment through orders. Use a preregistered seven-day purchase window and a later refund check; adjust the window to the buying cycle before launch. Anonymous or ambiguous origin remains unknown. Report observed-source coverage and all-channel guardrails. For discovery changes, use product clusters and order counts per assigned product-week to avoid conditioning on visitors the treatment itself created.

There is no supportable lift forecast here. Break-even requires additional retained orders multiplied by contribution per order to exceed the fee and delivery cost. Every proposed price is an unvalidated willingness-to-pay test. Zero and negative effects remain possible. Cost savings matter when they reduce delivery expense or redirect scarce effort into work that produces purchases; do not count the same recovered order under several interventions.
