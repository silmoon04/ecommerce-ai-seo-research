# Geoffy: a workflow benchmark for purchase-focused AI commerce

Research snapshot: **29 September 2026**. Geoffy merits selection for the detail of its publishing and operational workflow. The sources do not establish that it is a commercial winner, profitable business, or cause of incremental AI-origin purchases. No installation, account access or paid experiment was performed.

Three Shopify reviews were visible at retrieval. PadelDogs reports more traffic and sales after five months of use; another five-month reviewer describes clearer product discovery, while a three-day reviewer reports AI mentions. These are useful merchant experiences without a counterfactual, verified payment history or attributed order series. Geoffy also reports a customer's LLM referral share rising from 1% to 5% within three weeks. A changed traffic share does not establish additional orders and could change because its denominator fell. [Merchant reviews](https://apps.shopify.com/geoffy/reviews), [vendor outcome claim](https://geoffy.ai/geo-vs-seo).

**Current offer.** Optimise is available for Shopify and WordPress/WooCommerce; the documentation additionally supports custom Next.js and Astro storefronts. Monitor and Influence remain early access. The latter produces source-informed listing and outreach material for merchants to place. This already addresses work beyond the merchant's own pages. [Product](https://geoffy.ai/), [platform FAQ](https://geoffy.ai/docs/faq).

| Offer shown on the source | Entry | Middle | Highest |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Website monthly bundles, GBP | £199 | £499 | £1,499 |
| Shopify listing, USD per 30 days | $249 | $649 | $1,949 |

The website also lists standalone Optimise from £99/month and sales-assisted onboarding around a free score. The Shopify listing advertises 14-day trials. These are channel-specific offers, not currency equivalents or a guarantee of identical access. [Website pricing](https://geoffy.ai/pricing), [Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/geoffy).

**What is already implemented in the documented workflow.** Geoffy reads catalog records, leaves unavailable facts empty, prepares content for merchant review, publishes it and checks drift. Its factual restraint matters, but agreement with a catalog does not independently establish that a manufacturer claim is true. The workflow needs good source data to start with. [How it works](https://geoffy.ai/how-it-works).

Publication verification is particularly concrete: it fetches the public product page, detects stale versions and canonical problems, and checks that the namespace returns the correct site's artifacts in the right content type. A generic successful response is insufficient. This is already a competitor capability; building a server-HTML checker alone would not be a meaningful edge. [Checking it worked](https://geoffy.ai/docs/headless/nextjs/concepts/checking-it-worked).

The supplementary Product node deliberately excludes offers. The commerce platform owns price and availability, while Geoffy adds supporting attributes and suitability information. The implementation does not rewrite the existing page or schema. Marketing references to coherence should therefore not be read as an independent replacement for the platform's live commerce record. [FAQ](https://geoffy.ai/docs/faq).

**Runtimes and freshness.** The headless package requires Node 18 or later and ESM; React is optional outside its Next.js adapter. Its server component can decline a mismatched canonical path or language, and failure falls back to the underlying page. Language matching alone does not distinguish English markets: the reference explicitly permits en-GB content on en-US pages. That is not a guarantee of correct currency, shipping eligibility or local terms. [Package reference](https://geoffy.ai/docs/headless/nextjs/reference/package).

The normal cache window is one hour; a signed invalidation route makes published changes arrive faster. Static Astro builds require rebuilding and cannot supply the documented dynamic namespace in the same way. Those are storefront refresh mechanics, not commitments about external indexing or recommendation timing. [Next.js updates](https://geoffy.ai/docs/headless/nextjs/concepts/how-updates-arrive), [Astro quickstart](https://geoffy.ai/docs/headless/astro).

**What the page check cannot guarantee.** Correct HTML does not prove a destination accepted it, an engine retrieved it, a model interpreted it accurately, or a shopper received the correct variant at checkout. Shopify already offers preferred field sources and custom grouping; it describes these as delayed inputs rather than guarantees of placement. Google already repairs some offer discrepancies automatically but says that this does not replace regular data updates. [Shopify mappings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog/mapping), [Catalog limits](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog), [Google updates](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12157888?hl=en-GB).

OpenAI describes consumer shopping as context-dependent and acknowledges delays in updated price or shipping information. Its current feed specification requires publishing current price and availability when they change; date fields do not schedule those changes. A verified page and an accepted feed can still be behind a checkout's current reality. [Consumer shopping](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-shopping-with-chatgpt-search), [feed contract](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/file-upload/products).

**Harness capability assessment.**

| Layer | Public evidence | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Controlled offline replay | Not found | No documented frozen tool-response experiment located; absence of documentation is not proof of absence. |
| Live monitoring | Documented | Early-access scheduled engine checks; optional crawler/referral telemetry is a separate capability. |
| Randomized experiment | Unclear | Higher Shopify tiers advertise holdouts; assignment, power, contamination and endpoint are not disclosed. |
| Purchase measurement | Unclear | Examined tracking and benchmark docs do not establish order-level causal measurement. |

Geoffy's optional middleware reports AI crawlers and assistant-origin visits. Its specified payload includes timing, page path and source, with additional crawler verification data where available. It documents queue loss and sampling; this is not an order ledger. [AI visits](https://geoffy.ai/docs/headless/nextjs/guides/ai-visits), [holdout offer](https://apps.shopify.com/geoffy).

The garden-furniture benchmark is useful precisely because it distinguishes being cited from being named. Its 90 answers comprise ten prompts, three APIs and three repetitions; consumer-app examples are separate. It is an observational sample, not ninety independent human buying decisions or a treatment effect. [Benchmark and method](https://geoffy.ai/sectors/garden-furniture).

The shared proxy proposal can isolate sensitivity to a field or result position. It cannot reproduce proprietary retrieval, personalization or merchant ranking. Keep held-out intents independent of training examples, count repeated runs within intent, and label rank changes as hypothetical sensitivity. The shared conversation's percentage ladder and conversion-looking estimates are invented illustrations. They must not become sales claims. Burnish already advertises referral/order attribution and explicitly limits its lift to directional evidence; the competitive edge must go beyond adding an attribution dashboard. [Burnish's stated method](https://useburnish.com/what-it-does/).

**Ten purchase hypotheses.** These are buildable proposals or scoped integration bets, not measured effects. The companion JSON contains the complete data requirements, trial design, cost controls, test price and falsifier for each.

1. **Verify the purchasable offer after the destination accepts it.** Trace a specific variant and market from the source through publication, destination diagnostics and cart validation. Repair demonstrated disagreements. Compare randomized product families against existing controls using retained AI-origin orders per family-day. The distinction from existing mapping and HTML checks is the observed purchase blocker. Destination state may remain inaccessible. [Existing mapping](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog/mapping).

2. **Carry the selected variant intact from AI result to checkout.** Preserve the exact size, colour or pack across redirects, market changes and cart creation. Request a new choice if unavailable; never silently substitute. Native variant links already exist, so prove real handoff failures first. Randomize eligible arrivals and measure seven-day purchases, then returns. [Variant support](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/product-merchandising/variants).

3. **Answer the shopper's delivery deadline before the shortlist hardens.** Use voluntarily supplied destination and needed-by date to quote feasible stock and shipping, then revalidate at checkout. Test purchases and on-time retained orders against the current flow. Native delivery estimates are the baseline; inventing a promise is not an intervention. [Delivery features](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/setup/delivery-expectations/overview).

4. **Prove hard compatibility instead of describing generic suitability.** Convert authoritative dimensions, connector rules and exclusions into a supported/incompatible/unknown verdict for a buyer's configuration. Test against passive factual content. Geoffy already advocates using returns to improve facts; executable compatibility must show an additional retained-order benefit. [Geoffy's returns argument](https://geoffy.ai/blog/returns-data-is-a-content-brief).

5. **Sell the complete working solution within the stated budget.** Build the minimum compatible basket for the task, distinguish mandatory from optional parts and show delivered cost before commitment. Randomize against existing individual-product or bundle presentation. Count additional purchasing customers, not just larger baskets. Native bundles already cover basic assembly and component stock. [Bundle constraints](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/bundles/eligibility-and-considerations).

6. **Show the real cost and conditions of changing one's mind.** Explain the seller, delivery cost, return expense and relevant conditions for this basket and destination, using approved policies. Compare with existing compliant disclosures without withholding required information. Jurisdiction and category exceptions need professional review; the feature does not certify compliance. Test retained purchases and dispute costs. [Official UK guidance](https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses).

7. **Let shoppers resume a consented buying plan.** Preserve only the shortlist and practical requirements the user chooses to share, using a revocable opaque token. Refresh offers on return and keep private cart credentials out of links. Randomize the offer before opt-in and measure thirty-day purchases across everyone assigned, not only people who save. [Cart primitives and security constraints](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/building-with-the-storefront-api/cart/manage).

8. **Turn verified unmet constraints into a small real assortment test.** When independent shoppers repeatedly need an absent size or compatible component, distinguish a genuine supply gap from search failure. Test actual limited inventory or an honestly described preorder. Measure total category purchases and unsold-stock costs so displacement cannot masquerade as demand. Native search improvements should be tried when retrieval is the problem. [Existing search behavior](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/storefront-search/search-and-discovery-modify-search).

9. **Reduce the decision work imposed by the fact widget.** Compare a compact, accessible decision summary with the current layout while keeping the same approved facts available to humans and crawlers. Include decisive limitations and measure purchases, comprehension, returns and latency. More readable content is a hypothesis; widget engagement is not the purchase endpoint. [Existing widget](https://geoffy.ai/).

10. **Calibrate the replay harness against retained orders before buying more tests.** Promote offline candidates into randomized merchant trials, then prospectively compare calibrated allocation with equal-budget expert prioritization across stores. The causal value is better deployed decisions. Kill the claim if offline scores cannot predict held-out purchase effects or the allocation policy cannot outperform the comparator. [Existing holdout offer](https://apps.shopify.com/geoffy), [API benchmark limits](https://geoffy.ai/sectors/garden-furniture).

**Economics and test discipline.** No defensible percentage uplift was found for these proposals. As a deliberately illustrative break-even calculation, a £199 monthly fee requires eight additional retained orders at an assumed £25 contribution per order before implementation or operating cost. Neither the margin nor the extra orders is an estimate of actual performance.

Randomize visitors for post-arrival changes and product families or merchant clusters for discoverability changes. Predefine origin coverage, window, refunds, stock shocks and cross-product displacement. Keep unknown-origin purchases visible. Do not divide acquisition gains by a treatment-altered referral denominator and call that conversion improvement. Eight to twelve weeks is a planning window, not a promise of adequate power; sparse stores may require pooling, longer observation or an inconclusive verdict. Cost savings matter only if they preserve coverage and redirect work toward interventions that create retained purchases.
