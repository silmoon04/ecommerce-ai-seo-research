# Peec AI: turning monitored shopping demand into retained purchases

Snapshot: 29 September 2026. Commercial benchmark. This revisits the [prior monitoring notes](../../deep-dive/monitoring.md) against current primary sources and the [shared harness proposal](https://chatgpt.com/s/t_6abb72f6509c8191a0d80d6c1329760d). The [companion JSON](peec.json) contains ten complete idea records, experiment designs, cost controls and source definitions.

Peec is a credible benchmark because there is evidence of a paying market, and because its product already covers much of the obvious analytics work. Its May 2026 company announcement reports $10 million ARR and more than 2,500 customers. Those are company-reported figures, not audited revenue, profit, retention or customer ROI. They support investigating this buyer category; they do not establish a profitable business model for a new entrant. [Peec announcement](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/28/3303009/0/en/Peec-AI-hits-10M-ARR-16-months-after-launch-as-brands-race-to-optimize-for-AI-search.html)

The outcome evidence needs similar care. Peec's Merge case claims sevenfold growth in AI demo requests, tenfold growth in LLM traffic and sixfold higher conversion rates. The Mint Position case reports a 16% ChatGPT visibility improvement and a financing-deal anecdote. These are vendor-published B2B cases without a randomized ecommerce purchase counterfactual. None supplies a supportable conversion percentage for our proposed product. [Merge case](https://peec.ai/blog/how-merge-drove-a-7x-increase-in-demo-requests-from-llms-with-peec-ai), [Mint Position case](https://peec.ai/blog/how-mint-position-helps-fintech-clients-unlock-16-more-ai-search-visibility-and-secure-1-search-positions-with-peec-ai)

**What Peec already sells.** Its brand plans currently enumerate 50, 150 and 350 prompts with three models, plus custom Enterprise. The retrieved live pricing page did not expose numeric price cards. A separate vendor disclosure, dated July and stating confirmation on 10 July 2026, lists $95, $245 and $495 monthly. The retrieved disclosure does not explicitly name the dollar currency; these are dated vendor quotes, not verified September checkout prices. [Current pricing](https://peec.ai/pricing), [vendor price disclosure](https://peec.ai/ai-instructions)

Shopping Analytics imports a catalog and compares individual products, quoted prices, recommendation positions and merchant destinations. It also recommends product changes. Its documentation says the shopping analysis currently uses ChatGPT and reuses the chats Peec already monitors. This is meaningful overlap with a proposed SKU analytics product. The wording of win-rate denominators differs between launch copy and the product table, so metric comparisons need an explicit denominator. [Shopping launch](https://peec.ai/blog/ai-shopping-analytics), [shopping overview](https://docs.peec.ai/overview), [product definitions](https://docs.peec.ai/products)

The current plan table also lists agent actions, custom skills and a visibility lift predictor. Actions have acceptance and completion states. Their Impact view places status markers on subsequent visibility, sentiment, position and share-of-voice charts. Therefore, an action dashboard or before/after chart is weak differentiation. The reviewed documentation does not establish randomized purchase experiments. [Pricing capabilities](https://peec.ai/pricing), [Actions documentation](https://docs.peec.ai/actions)

The research distinction matters: Peec explicitly cautions that its open-reranker results do not identify a proprietary production model or guarantee a citation. Its separate name experiment is evidence that the vendor conducts controlled research, not evidence of a merchant purchase-testing service. [Reranker research](https://peec.ai/blog/rerankers-for-geo-aeo-how-ai-search-chooses-passages-and-sources), [name study](https://peec.ai/blog/ai-assistants-judge-your-company-by-its-name-i-measured-how-much), [collection method](https://docs.peec.ai/intro-to-peec-ai)

**Purchase reporting has an exactness boundary.** Peec's “Conversions” can be any configured GA4 key event, including a signup. Purchase revenue requires appropriate instrumentation. Missing referrers can appear as Direct, and Google AI traffic can remain Organic Search. A product must label recorded AI-origin purchases as a measured subset. It cannot quietly assign every unexplained order to AI. [AI Referrals documentation](https://docs.peec.ai/ai-referrals)

“My website” joins bot logs, sampled source use and analytics by page path. Those are three independent datasets, not one person's trace from a particular answer to an order. Peec also features a community Amplitude attribution workflow, so joining revenue into reports is already part of the competitive baseline. [My website documentation](https://docs.peec.ai/my-website), [attribution workflow](https://peec.ai/mcp-use-cases/ai-visibility-to-revenue-attribution)

For our experiments, the merchant order ledger should determine whether payment completed, which variant sold, whether it was canceled, and whether it was returned. GA4 purchase and refund events have transaction/item identifiers that can support reconciliation, but event collection alone does not establish a real fulfilled order. Keep observed source evidence, shopper-declared origin and unknown origin separate. Report retained purchases and contribution after refunds; never replace purchases with add-to-cart events. [GA4 ecommerce specification](https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/collection/ga4/ecommerce)

**Use the proposed proxy as a diagnostic instrument.** Custom function calling lets our application provide tool results. Freeze the retrieved candidate set, vary one deployable intervention, and record discovery, correct understanding, shortlist and recommendation separately. Built-in web search can expose consulted sources, but neither API facility documents exact reproduction of consumer ChatGPT shopping. [Function calling](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/function-calling), [web search](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/tools-web-search)

The shared conversation's percentages are invented examples. Fifty reruns of the same shopper specification do not supply fifty independent human intents. Moving a merchant from fourth to second is an oracle sensitivity test unless a real intervention can achieve that change. Use varied real intent samples, preserve uncertainty between intent clusters, and reserve held-out intents for transfer checks. Consumer shopping also incorporates context, including Memory, and multiple metadata sources. A synthetic recommendation probability must retain its simulation label. [OpenAI shopping explanation](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-shopping-with-chatgpt-search)

The next layer is merchant experimentation. Randomize stable shoppers for post-arrival experiences; randomize product families or inventory pools when interventions change discovery or share scarce stock. A denominator created by treatment, such as increased referral traffic, can distort discovery conversion comparisons. Measure assigned-unit purchase counts, spillovers, unknown origin and returns over a prespecified buying cycle. Live monitoring is an external check, not the counterfactual.

The ten purchase mechanisms below are intentionally different. Their numeric impact ranges remain unestimated.

1. **Choose changes by randomized order lift.** Add an order ledger and experiment registry around Peec's action workflow, then compare purchase-tested prioritization with ordinary action prioritization at equal implementation budget. Randomize independent merchant-category blocks and count retained AI-attributable orders per block-week, with total orders as a guardrail. This enabling tool creates value only when it redirects work toward changes that really sell more. Too few independent blocks or unstable source coverage kills the causal claim. Its overlap with [Peec Actions](https://docs.peec.ai/actions) is explicit.

2. **Preserve the recommended variant.** When an incoming link supplies verifiable variant intent, carry the selected size, color and model through PDP rendering and buyer-confirmed cart creation. Randomize eligible shoppers against current behavior and count retained purchases, wrong-variant returns and latency. Shopify already supplies the [deep-link mechanism](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/product-merchandising/variants). The value would come from repairing observed AI handoff mismatches. Do not infer a choice from an ambiguous product-family link.

3. **Recover stale recommendations with constraint-safe substitutes.** When the recommended item is unavailable, offer a small set that satisfies buyer-confirmed hard requirements, with differences visible. Compare against the merchant's existing related-products or waitlist experience. Count any retained purchase across the substitute family, rather than celebrating a SKU switch. [Shopify's existing recommendations](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/storefront-search/search-and-discovery-recommendations) make generic substitution a crowded feature; verified fit and outcome measurement need to earn their cost.

4. **Carry a buyer-approved comparison brief into the store.** A cooperating assistant could hand over selected products and non-sensitive requirements through an expiring opaque handle. Randomize before the sharing offer, so refusals and permission friction remain in the purchase denominator. This is a proposed partner extension, not something available from a normal referrer. [UCP identity linking](https://ucp.dev/2026-04-08/specification/identity-linking/) supplies authorization concepts, but does not establish this brief format. No partner reach means no viable product.

5. **Answer the real delivery deadline.** For shoppers who voluntarily specify a required-by date before assignment, compare the standard accurate shipping estimate with an explicit deadline-fit answer and eligible alternatives. Count purchases that arrive on time and remain retained. Use current warehouse capacity, carrier calendars and conservative estimates. [Google's shipping schema](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/shipping-policy) already supports timing facts; external-engine uptake remains uncertain. More reassuring copy without reliable fulfillment could increase refunds.

6. **Resolve one decisive compatibility doubt.** For an already-shortlisted technical product, ask one question that can change the suitability verdict, then show verified supporting facts. Compare with the merchant's current quiz or specification table and count retained suitable purchases. [RevenueHunt](https://revenuehunt.com/guided-selling-quiz/) already offers compatibility quizzes. The hypothesis is that a short residual-doubt check works better at this stage, not that guided selling is new. Incomplete specifications must produce uncertainty rather than reassurance.

7. **Recover demand through an available authorized seller.** Let a manufacturer offer verified regional sellers when direct fulfillment fails. Compare against its current locator and reconcile both direct and participating-retailer receipts. Count total retained brand purchases, explicitly subtracting mere channel substitution. [PriceSpider](https://www.pricespider.com/where-to-buy/) already combines retailer routing and tracking. The proposed specialization needs AI-arrival outcomes and strong receipt coverage; otherwise an integration with the incumbent may be preferable.

8. **Give a usable total-price answer before commitment.** Show a destination-specific payable estimate from the merchant's existing rules and reconcile it with checkout. Compare two compliant presentations; never withhold required charges in a control. [UK CMA guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/price-transparency-cma209/providing-clear-and-accurate-information-about-prices-summary) addresses mandatory charges, while [Shopify Markets](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/markets/customizations/duties-and-taxes) already supplies much of the calculation. Count retained international purchases and complaints. Accurate pricing can legitimately reduce conversion where the offer is poor.

9. **Offer a real short stock hold.** After an explicit buyer selection, a supported merchant integration could reserve a unit through the handoff. [Shopify's draft-order API](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2026-01/payloads/DraftOrderCreatePayload) demonstrates a reservation mechanism, not ordinary-cart support. Randomize inventory pools or time blocks with washout, and count total retained orders across channels. If every unit would sell anyway, more AI orders can merely displace other buyers. Never reserve inventory for passive bot retrieval.

10. **Resume a failed agent checkout.** After a common gateway detects an eligible error, randomize current valid recovery against improved state retrieval and buyer handoff. Preserve the cart, honor required review, and deduplicate actual paid orders. [UCP already specifies continuation and idempotent recovery](https://ucp.dev/latest/specification/shopping/checkout/); a new product must improve real implementation outcomes. Synthetic fault replay is useful validation, but creates no revenue. Duplicate purchases or lost authorization kill the proposal.

**Economics and first pilot.** The JSON records unvalidated GBP test prices, not inferred demand. Every idea uses a break-even model: additional retained purchases times realized contribution, less implementation, operations and subscription costs. Zero and negative results remain possible. Do not add the ten mechanisms' gains together; many affect the same shopper.

Start with a merchant that already has measurable AI referrals and an observable downstream failure. Variant preservation or compatibility checking can reach a real purchase experiment with fewer partners than portable briefs or retailer networks. Keep Peec as a possible data source. Use deterministic identity and order joins, inventory-triggered checks, and replay only where it can guide a deployable change. That reduces cost while preserving the primary objective: additional retained purchases.
