# Scrunch / Sitecore AXP: from agent-readable pages to purchases

Research snapshot: 29 September 2026. Commercial benchmark. Full intervention specifications, ten experiments, costs, falsifiers and source records are in [scrunch.json](scrunch.json).

Scrunch is a credible benchmark for an enterprise product that both diagnoses AI discovery problems and changes what agents receive. It does not establish that serving a shorter page causes a predictable increase in purchases. The stronger opportunity for a new product is to resolve specific failures between a recommendation and a completed, suitable order, using Scrunch or native infrastructure where it already works.

The current direct pricing page lists Starter at $250/month with annual billing or $300 monthly, Growth at $417 annually or $500 monthly, and custom Enterprise. Starter/Growth include 350/700 custom prompts, 3/5 seats and 5/10 page audits. The page uses dollar symbols without an explicit currency code. Older indexed Core tables conflict with this direct rendering; they are not combined here. AXP is a separate product, and a public AXP-only price was not found. [Pricing](https://scrunch.com/pricing/), [AXP help](https://helpcenter.scrunchai.com/en/articles/13656392-agent-experience-platform-axp).

AXP delivers approved, simplified HTML through the merchant's existing edge infrastructure. Agent citations retain the normal public URL, so people still arrive at the human site. Content upload, deployment logs and version redeployment are documented. Freshness depends on configuration: a live-page mode can reoptimize updates, while stored content needs a refresh process. This is an existing implementation workflow, including controls that a new entrant should not claim to have invented. [Delivery behavior](https://helpcenter.scrunchai.com/en/articles/12845899-how-axp-serves-content-to-ai-agents), [Deployment guide](https://scrunch.com/how-tos/how-to-serve-ai-optimized-content-directly-to-llms/), [Freshness modes](https://scrunch.com/faqs/how-does-scrunchs-axp-approach-compare-to-publishing-ai-friendly-content-directly-in-the-cms).

Scrunch also advertises product-level recommendations, rank, retailer destinations and prompt analysis. Its GA4 connection reports transactions and AI purchase revenue when configured. Neither retailer visibility nor referral revenue is an uncovered category. [Shopping](https://scrunch.com/platform/shopping/), [GA4 documentation](https://helpcenter.scrunchai.com/en/articles/11826932-connecting-the-ai-referrals-tool-to-your-google-analytics-account).

## What the evidence supports

Sitecore confirmed acquiring Scrunch on 3 June 2026 and cited adoption by more than 500 brands and agencies. This is evidence of enterprise distribution and strategic value. It does not disclose Scrunch's standalone profit, retention or unit economics. [Acquisition announcement](https://www.sitecore.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/2026/06/sitecore-acquires-scrunch-to-help-brands-influence-discovery--and-buying-decisions).

Akamai compared AXP pages with non-AXP pages between 1 December 2025 and 19 January 2026. Its case reports 85% more citations, 38% more prompts with citations and 364% higher brand presence for associated non-branded prompts. It separately gives 22% better citation performance since launch. The acquisition release uses 218% citation growth. These scopes are not reconciled publicly and must not be merged. The case does not disclose randomized allocation, sample sizes or purchases. It supports a deployed mechanism and an associated visibility improvement. [Akamai case](https://scrunch.com/case-studies/akamai-5x-brand-presence-in-ai-search-results/).

Runpod is closer to a commercial outcome: the vendor case reports fourfold growth in new paying customers over 90 days, after prompt expansion, content changes and rendering repairs. It reports 2,100 conversions from roughly 28,000 visitors as about 8%, and software spend falling from $3,000 to $500/month. The case lacks a randomized control and does not isolate AXP. Its result cannot become an expected ecommerce uplift. [Runpod case](https://scrunch.com/news/2025-07-how-runpod-leveraged-the-scrunch-ai-platform-to-achieve-4x-growth%2C-turning-chatgpt-into-a-top-performing-acquisition-channel-).

There is an important revision in Scrunch's own research. The 31 August 2026 version of *From Prompt to Purchase* estimates increases of 4.3 percentage points in brand searches, 2.4 in brand-site visits and 1.0 in retailer-page visits for recommendations to users without recent observed engagement. The paper explicitly calls the design observational and says transactions were not observed. It also finds no reliable mention-position gradient, unlike the stronger language in the June blog. Its measurement pipeline produces reports and is not described as a standalone customer feature. Use the revised paper, not the older blog's figures, for this assessment. [Paper v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.10907v2), [Earlier blog](https://scrunch.com/blog/prompt-to-purchase-pipeline-how-ai-influences-buyer-behavior).

## Where the proposed harness fits

Live monitoring is documented: Scrunch collects responses through browser automation and official APIs. Public documentation for controlled offline tool-response replay or a randomized purchase experiment was not found in the sources and targeted searches reviewed. That is a search finding, not proof those capabilities are absent. [Collection methods](https://scrunch.com/faqs/what-methods-does-scrunch-use-to-collect-data-from-ai-platforms).

A custom `search_products` tool can support the shared conversation's frozen-result experiment. It answers a bounded question: given this model, tool contract, intent and retrieved set, did an altered fact change selection? It does not reproduce consumer ChatGPT retrieval, private context or merchant ranking. Fifty reruns of one intent are repeated stochastic observations, not fifty independent shoppers. Moving an item up a frozen result list tests sensitivity to position; it does not show that a merchant can earn that position. The shared conversation's percentage ladders are invented illustrations. [Custom tools](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/tools), [Actual ChatGPT shopping inputs](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-shopping-with-chatgpt-search).

Keep four records: offline ablation, live retrieval/answer observation, randomized merchant intervention, and verified paid orders. For discovery changes, randomize stable product-family or category clusters because caches can mix request-level treatments. Use order counts with fixed preperiod demand adjustment, not only conversion rates among the different visitors each treatment attracts. For landing/checkout changes, randomize shoppers before the intervention and include everyone assigned. Track returns and purchases across the merchant's own products to detect substitution.

Native competition limits a CDN-first strategy. Cloudflare already converts negotiated requests to Markdown. Shopify provides source mapping, grouping and product distribution and advises against placing a proxy in front of Shopify stores. Build through supported feeds, CMS templates, AXP or partner APIs according to the observed failure. [Cloudflare conversion](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/markdown-for-agents/), [Shopify mapping](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog/mapping), [Shopify discovery](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/products).

## Ten distinct interventions

1. **Constraint-preserving agent page compiler.** For technical catalogs, preserve units, exclusions and exact variant scope when compressing source facts. The path is fewer suitability mistakes, better qualified selection, then retained purchases. This builds on AXP/Cloudflare delivery; the proposed advantage is measured preservation under truncation. Randomize product-family deployments against current approved output. Kill it if extraction improves but independently powered purchase results do not. [Existing compression](https://scrunch.com/platform/agent-experience/).

2. **Retired-citation rescue for sold-out variants.** Maintain historical item identities and truthful unavailable-item pages. Offer current substitutes that satisfy the old variant's explicit constraints, with user choice rather than a silent redirect. Native inventory updates cannot recall every old recommendation. Randomize affected arrivals against the current unavailable-page experience; count any retained merchant purchase, not substitute clicks. It fails where acceptable substitutes rarely exist. [Freshness modes](https://scrunch.com/faqs/how-does-scrunchs-axp-approach-compare-to-publishing-ai-friendly-content-directly-in-the-cms).

3. **Carry the shortlist into the product page.** An owned assistant or authorized partner passes a short-lived token containing the chosen variant and a user-approved checklist. The landing page restores these choices and flags changed facts. This reduces repeated work. Selected-variant URLs already exist; private ChatGPT chats are not shared with merchants. Randomize the optional handoff before acceptance and measure purchases among everyone assigned. Partner access is a dependency. [Feed identity](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/file-upload/products), [Chat privacy](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12911370-using-shopping-r).

4. **Quote the complete delivered price before recommendation.** A read-only service calculates a destination-specific payable total and revalidates it at checkout. Fewer surprises could increase fulfilled purchases despite lower clickthrough. UCP already provides fulfillment primitives. Compare earlier accurate quoting with a compliant existing disclosure flow, never with hidden mandatory charges. UK CMA guidance supports the information requirement, not a legal guarantee for the proposed software. [UCP fulfillment](https://ucp.dev/specification/shopping/extensions/fulfillment/), [CMA guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/price-transparency-cma209/providing-clear-and-accurate-information-about-prices-summary).

5. **Compatibility answers that execute against a parts graph.** For accessories and replacement parts, query verified model/revision relationships instead of guessing from descriptions. Return compatible, incompatible or unknown, with evidence. Correct confidence can turn hesitation into a kept purchase. True Fit already addresses apparel fit through APIs/MCP, so choose a narrower non-apparel domain. Test retained purchasing users and error coverage; a high answer accuracy achieved through excessive abstention is insufficient. [True Fit specification](https://www.truefit.com/fit-intelligence-spec).

6. **Keep earned benefits when the shopper changes agents.** Optional account linking restores existing loyalty eligibility and saved choices without inventing discounts. The purchase mechanism is reduced re-entry and preserved value for returning shoppers. UCP already specifies consented identity linking; the work is a reliable merchant integration at the right moment. Randomize before linking and measure 30-day purchases and contribution, including guest shoppers. Authentication friction or mere order acceleration can erase the benefit. [Identity linking](https://ucp.dev/2026-04-08/specification/identity-linking/).

7. **Route unavailable brand products to a buyable authorized retailer.** Reconcile exact SKU, live stock and destination eligibility when D2C cannot fulfill. Let the shopper choose among verified retailer offers. PriceSpider already supplies substantial routing, inventory and purchase-tracking infrastructure; integrate it rather than inventing that category again. Compare with the existing retailer list and measure total retained brand purchases across participating sellers. Missing retailer outcomes remain unknown, not zero sales. [Where to Buy](https://www.pricespider.com/where-to-buy/), [Tracking limits](https://www.pricespider.com/Blog/how-pricespiders-universal-tracking-suite-works/).

8. **Purchase panel that calibrates the replay score.** Connect distinct real shopping intents, consented exposure records and merchant transactions to intervention versions. Freeze the resulting selection policy, then compare equal-budget deployment portfolios across randomized merchant-category clusters. Its causal role is better allocation of work; a dashboard alone creates no purchases. The primary result is retained orders per implementation dollar. Reject the model if held-out simulation rankings fail to predict live order effects. [Research gap](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.10907v2).

9. **Show the decisive tradeoff with verifiable evidence.** Let shoppers choose a concern, then display condition-specific product evidence, limitations and a compact comparison. Use actual manufacturer or licensed testing; commission missing tests only where demand justifies them. Native shopping research already gives tradeoffs, so the addition must be defensible evidence and better comprehension. Randomize against existing approved comparison content and count retained purchasers. Higher trust scores without orders fail the commercial claim. [Existing buyer guides](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12911370-using-shopping-r).

10. **Build a complete, removable project kit.** Turn a defined job into a minimum compatible basket, subtracting what the shopper says they already owns. Separate required and optional components and show the delivered total before confirmation. This can resolve uncertainty that prevents buying the main item. Existing bundling is substantial competition. Test whether more users complete a retained project purchase; higher accessory revenue or basket value alone does not satisfy the hypothesis. [Existing bundles](https://www.pricespider.com/where-to-buy/), [Partner checkout access](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/guides/product-checkout-conversion-spec).

Every proposed lift is unmeasured. The JSON gives unvalidated test prices and transparent break-even arithmetic, not forecasts. For example, US$300/month requires ten extra retained orders at US$30 contribution each before implementation costs. Test each intervention separately; their affected shoppers and purchases overlap, so their benefits cannot be added. The strongest initial candidates are unavailable-variant recovery and exact requirement handoff where a merchant can show substantial affected traffic. Compatibility and project completeness merit integration investment when merchants own authoritative data. The calibrated purchase panel is the more ambitious research product, with a higher data and sample-size burden.
