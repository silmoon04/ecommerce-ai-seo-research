# Shopify native agentic commerce benchmark

Research snapshot: 29 September 2026. Benchmark type: native. The companion [JSON](shopify.json) contains ten complete proposals, experiment definitions, failure conditions and the source register.

Shopify is a demanding baseline for a new AI discovery/conversion product because it already connects product data, agent-facing commerce tools, checkout and order records. That position makes it a useful benchmark; it does not establish a superior causal conversion rate or the profitability of any standalone AI feature. Its documented capabilities rule out treating source mapping, FAQs, shopper simulation or an A/B dashboard as an unexplored product category. [Shopify agentic storefronts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts)

**What merchants already receive.** Catalog Mapping accepts preferred title, description and category sources, including metafields and metaobjects, and supports custom variant grouping. Configurations influence Catalog with a processing delay; they do not guarantee a particular downstream representation. Shopify also serves `/agents.md`, `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt` itself. A new app needs more than generating those files. [Catalog Mapping](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog/mapping), [Catalog discovery](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/products)

The Agentic admin includes raw Catalog search previews, suggested queries, listing insights and channel performance. Shopify explicitly cautions that AI channels can rerank results. Knowledge Base supplies generated/custom FAQs, unanswered-question logs and test queries with matched resources. Its help page says it improves response accuracy without increasing how often a store appears. [Agentic management](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/agentic-home), [Knowledge Base](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/knowledge-base), [FAQ testing](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/knowledge-base/managing-faqs)

Knowledge Base is free on all Shopify plans. Sidekick analyzes data, edits products, generates content and builds admin apps with changes presented for review. Its generated apps cannot supply customer-visible theme, checkout or customer-account applications. “An AI assistant that recommends improvements” is already a native offer. [AI optimization guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/seo/optimizing-store-for-ai), [Sidekick](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/ai-powered-tools/sidekick), [Generated-app limits](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/ai-powered-tools/sidekick/generate-apps)

Availability must be stated per route:

| Channel | Documented current route and eligibility |
| --- | --- |
| ChatGPT | Discovery referral to the merchant's full checkout, in an in-app browser or new tab. Merchant must sell to US customers; it can be based elsewhere. [Source](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/chatgpt) |
| Google AI Mode/Gemini | Direct checkout is rolling out for US-based merchants selling to US buyers. Discovery uses Merchant Center. Other buyers can be redirected to the store. [Source](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/google) |
| Microsoft Copilot | Direct checkout for eligible merchants selling to US buyers, with a storefront option. [Source](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/microsoft) |
| Meta | Direct checkout for eligible US, Canadian and Mexican buyers; unsupported information collection can trigger a handoff with the cart preserved. Shoppable ads have additional Meta-channel requirements. [Source](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/meta) |

These channel pages state no extra selling fee beyond standard payment processing. Product types, delivery methods and checkout extensions remain constrained by route. Google and Copilot, for example, do not support direct purchase of subscriptions, bundles or customizable products. Merchant eligibility is not universal feature availability. Meta's channel and overview/privacy pages differ about product-source routes, so a specific merchant's configuration needs verification rather than a blanket feed assumption. [Google constraints](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/google), [Data sharing](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/data-privacy)

External merchants on the Agentic plan need their own setup and identity verification; direct checkout starts disabled. Their ChatGPT purchases happen on their existing store, and Shopify does not provide that ChatGPT order history. This is a material measurement boundary. [Agentic-plan setup](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/agentic-plan-setup)

**The testing overlap is substantial.** SimGym already runs AI shoppers on live/draft Liquid themes and compares simulated add-to-cart behavior. It is an AI Research Preview requiring Shopify Network Intelligence and a store outside private mode; headless stores are excluded. After its monthly allowance, the documented price is USD 0.75 for a single-theme simulation and USD 1.50 for a comparison. Its results can differ from real buyers. [SimGym](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/simgym)

Rollouts supplies control/treatment experiments for Grow and higher plans, covering supported theme, checkout/account and catalog changes. Its analytics include purchase-related metrics but cover specified online-store/cart/checkout traffic, have fixed metrics and exclude some other storefront experiences. Smart Pricing separately documents randomized 50/50 price experiments for qualifying US stores in early access. Test prices apply to the online store, not other channels. Neither is evidence of arbitrary randomization inside ChatGPT or Google discovery. [Rollout types](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/markets/rollouts/rollout-types), [Rollout analytics](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/markets/rollouts/analytics), [Smart Pricing experiments](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/details/product-pricing/smart-pricing/create-experiment)

Public UCP tools support catalog search, carts, checkout handoff and authorized order access. Existing Shop identity can be exchanged for scoped buyer-linked tokens. However, the specific Global Catalog documentation labels personalized search “coming soon,” despite broader statements in the authentication overview. Universal Cart has an early-access waitlist. These are integration opportunities with access conditions, not capabilities to assume are universally live. [UCP overview](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents), [Buyer-linked tokens](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/profiles/buyer-linked-tokens), [Global Catalog](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/catalog/global-catalog), [Authentication tiers](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/profiles/auth-and-rate-limiting)

A buyer-linked identity is not blanket purchase authority or consent to marketing. Channel data access is scoped, and public order tools cover orders placed through the authorized agent. UCP order webhooks currently require registration through a Shopify partner manager; Shopify says there is no self-serve subscription API for that interface. A merchant app's authorized order access is a different integration route. [Data privacy](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/data-privacy), [Order MCP](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/orders/order-mcp), [Order webhooks](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/orders/order-webhooks)

**What the evidence can establish.** Shopify reports real channel-attributed orders and sales, combining referrals and direct checkout. Online-store conversion divides sessions completing checkout by sessions; that denominator does not represent all AI impressions. Headless agentic analytics is unavailable, and ordinary client-side pixels do not fire in supported direct checkouts. Attribution can identify a recorded route without proving that an intervention caused an additional purchase. [Agentic reporting](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/agentic-home), [Copilot measurement limits](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/microsoft)

The shared search-proxy proposal remains useful for mechanism tests: freeze the candidates, change one truthful field, and observe recommendation differences. It cannot recreate a proprietary consumer engine's retrieval, personalization or checkout. Fifty reruns estimate variability conditional on that simulated intent, not fifty independent shoppers. Moving a result upward measures sensitivity to privileged ranking, not an achievable ranking intervention. The shared 12%-67% ladder and 18.4%-31.7% example are invented illustrations.

Use four separate records: simulated decisions, observations of permitted live experiences, randomized merchant assignments and actual paid orders reconciled through returns. Randomize before the stage being changed. A discovery intervention needs an upstream unit or merchant/product cluster design; measuring only visitors who arrived can conceal changes to who was discovered. A landing experiment can randomize arriving shoppers but cannot establish increased discovery. None of the sources here establishes a transferable lift percentage.

The ten proposals below deliberately start beyond that baseline. Each is a hypothesis; the JSON specifies its buyer, comparator, power caveat, denominator, cost controls and falsifier.

1. **Calibrate simulator decisions against paid orders.** Learn which synthetic signals predict held-out merchant experiment outcomes, then abstain where transfer fails. Compare experiment-selection policies at equal engineering budgets across merchant decision cycles. The endpoint is retained AI-originated purchases, not a better simulation score. This extends existing simulation and testing through validated allocation of work. [Native comparator](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/simgym)

2. **Choose a valid purchase route before failure.** A merchant-specific capability manifest checks the actual cart and chooses a permitted direct route or prefilled storefront handoff. Shopify already supplies escalation; the proposed advantage is proactive conformance. Randomize missions inside a cooperative agent and measure paid purchases, including alternative routes. Arbitrary control over consumer AI checkout is not assumed. [Cart and checkout](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/carts-and-checkout)

3. **Offer a verified arrival plan.** For a buyer-provided deadline, combine inventory, warehouse cutoffs and carrier evidence into an expiring fulfillment plan. Compare it with accurate ordinary estimates on on-time retained purchases. Separate shipment promises from delivery estimates and underwrite guarantees only when real. This targets deadline uncertainty rather than richer shipping copy. [Native checkout context](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/carts-and-checkout/checkout-mcp)

4. **Prove a substitute fits.** In a narrow parts/accessories vertical, verify model-specific compatibility against authoritative records before recommending an available alternative. Measure compatible retained orders and false-fit returns. Shopify's existing recommendations and inferred Catalog specifications make “another recommender” a weak claim; licensed evidence and deterministic qualification are the proposed advantage. [Recommendations](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/product-merchandising/recommendations), [Inferred Catalog fields](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/catalog/global-catalog)

5. **Carry an editable purchase brief.** Let consenting buyers reuse explicit constraints, rejected options and expiry across cooperating agents. Existing login continuity does not establish portable current intent. Randomize returning buyers and count retained purchases per buyer over time, including those who never start a new mission. This is a frontier integration because platform cooperation and revocation matter. [Existing identity mechanism](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/profiles/buyer-linked-tokens)

6. **Recover delayed orders with a truthful choice.** Add verified available substitutes to timely delay/refund notices, preserving unrestricted cancellation and requiring explicit acceptance. Compare retained fulfilled orders against the same legally sufficient notice without alternatives. This preserves purchases after checkout; it is not acquisition uplift. The FTC source governs shipment promises and delay/refund duties in its stated US scope, not blanket approval of the design. [FTC rule](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule)

7. **Optimize a complete shopping mission.** Compare a few executable baskets under the buyer's total budget, required components and delivery constraints. Native carts and the Universal Cart roadmap already cover aggregation. The proposed edge is whole-basket feasibility. Count completed retained baskets per randomized mission, so splitting a basket into more orders cannot masquerade as growth. [Native cart options](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/carts-and-checkout)

8. **Preserve verified decision reasons at handoff.** With buyer approval, send a compact receipt linking the exact variant to confirmed requirements, factual evidence and accepted tradeoffs. Revalidate it on the landing page and permit correction. Test retained purchases against the same ordinary product page. The merchant receives only an authorized brief, not a private ChatGPT conversation. [Current referral route](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/chatgpt)

9. **Honor a valid quoted price automatically.** Recognize merchant-issued price receipts across cooperative AI handoffs and apply the existing price-honor policy without support intervention. Compare automatic and manual handling at identical eligible prices. Discounts and price locks already exist; provenance and friction reduction are the extra hypothesis. Never manufacture stale quotes to create an apparent problem. [Existing draft-order controls](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/create-orders/create-draft)

10. **Evaluate honest stock holds across the inventory pool.** Use buyer-requested, bounded holds without artificial urgency. Shopify already reserves draft-order inventory. Randomize SKU/inventory-pool periods and count all-channel retained purchases to detect displacement: a held buyer's successful purchase may simply replace another buyer's purchase. The opportunity survives only if total fulfilled demand grows. [Existing reservations](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/create-orders/create-draft)

Prices proposed in the JSON are unvalidated willingness-to-pay tests, not demand evidence. Every impact model permits zero or negative value. For any candidate, contribution from experimentally supported incremental retained orders must exceed implementation, operating and fulfillment costs. Low-cost native tools should be reused when they help; differentiation must come from an identifiable purchase failure and credible evidence that the added intervention repairs it.
