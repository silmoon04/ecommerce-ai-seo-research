# Screening the 260 candidates

The selected 50 are product hypotheses with plausible deployment routes, not 50 proven competitive advantages. Every raw candidate was reviewed, including the late Constructor, SearchPilot, Gorgias and psychological records. The final map contains 50 primary candidates, 169 merged candidates, 40 reserves and one rejected standalone workflow. The 219 primary or merged references occur once each in the selection plan; all 260 candidates occur once in the deduplication map. The portfolio covers all eight lenses and includes one pure measurement or allocation product, I50.

An idea survived when it named a purchase failure, a concrete intervention, a buyer who could authorize it, obtainable data, a competent competing workflow and an order endpoint. Existing capabilities could support an idea, but could not constitute its claimed edge. For example, an executable compatibility check needs a source-backed relationship and an observed buying question; another generated specification paragraph is insufficient. Integration-heavy proposals remain conditional on merchant systems, partner permissions and operating capacity. None assumes privileged access to a consumer assistant's private ranking or conversation.

The primary success criterion is additional actual purchases in a predeclared AI-origin cohort. Report completed purchases and purchasing customers, then mature cancellations, returns, fraud and contribution. A rise in retained orders caused entirely by fewer returns is useful, but must not be presented as more purchase events. Higher basket value, add-on attachment, retailer clickthrough, simulation preference and payment approval rates also cannot substitute for the primary endpoint. Known external referrals, authenticated partner handoffs and merchant-owned assistant use need separate strata; unknown origin stays unknown.

Candidates were grouped by intervention and failure, rather than their titles. Exact-variant rendering is distinct from compatible project assembly; the former preserves an already chosen item, while the latter determines whether the basket can perform a task. Temporary stockout substitution is distinct from explaining a discontinued model's successor. A delivery deadline is distinct from the remaining usable life of a perishable item. Known correctable checkout errors, missing admissible risk context and an ambiguous payment outcome remain three separate problems.

Broad candidates were not forced into every related slot. The general constraint compiler, concurrent-cart reconciliation, regional inventory transfers, generic fit enrichment and debugging traces remain reserves where their actuators differ from the selected idea. Merged records provide supporting variants or components; they are not instructions to build every component in an initial release. I50 contains ordinary assignment, falsification, pooling and allocation methods so those methods do not fill the idea quota.

Competition materially changed the screening. Shopify already provides synthetic theme comparison and live rollouts; Lily Max advertises an optimization loop; SearchPilot covers GEO, Merchant Center and full-funnel testing; Constructor already supports multi-item goals, contextual conversation and purchase measurement. The remaining claim is that a particular intervention or selection policy beats a competent deployment of these tools. Missing public documentation remains an unknown about private capabilities. [SimGym](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/simgym), [Shopify Rollouts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/markets/rollouts/rollout-types), [Lily Max](https://www.lily.ai/how-it-works/), [SearchPilot](https://www.searchpilot.com/ai), [Constructor agent documentation](https://docs.constructor.com/docs/products-ai-shopping-agents-learn-about-ai-shopping-agent).

The first eight define concrete build options. Their order expresses current preference, conditional on the merchant having the relevant failure and enough eligible traffic.

1. **I01: preserve the chosen variant.** Build one resolver from the incoming supported identifier to server-rendered selection, image, price and cart line. Add probes for the merchant's actual redirect and locale paths. Compare it with correctly configured native links, not a deliberately broken control. Randomize eligible arrivals before handling the variant and measure paid purchasers; audit wrong-item orders and latency. Stop if the supposedly lost selection was never observable or the defect is too rare to fund the connector.

2. **I02: recover a real stockout.** Build a small approved substitution table for one product family, combine it with live stock and show the buyer the material differences. Keep the original item and wait option visible. Test against the merchant's tuned related-products and waitlist flow. Follow the whole buying window so an earlier substitute does not masquerade as an extra eventual purchase. Expand only after suitable replacement purchases outweigh returns, margin losses and displaced later orders.

3. **I03: prove exact compatibility.** Start with one accessory category and a maintained manufacturer model/version table. Return compatible, incompatible or unknown with the governing source, and preserve the verified variant through checkout. Compare with an existing fitment finder or well-configured specifications flow. The endpoint is additional appropriate purchases among all assigned shoppers, with false-compatible decisions as a stop condition. Data licensing and expert maintenance are part of delivery cost.

4. **I04: resolve a real deadline.** Connect one fulfilment system to a postcode-and-needed-by checker. Return only operationally feasible shipping or collection options and revalidate the selected plan. Compare with the same accurate existing estimates at the normal decision point. Count additional paid orders and verify on-time fulfilment. This requires warehouse cutoffs, service calendars and actual stock locations; a model-generated date cannot replace those inputs.

5. **I05: prove that the basket completes the task.** Build a bounded dependency graph for a single project, such as a supported equipment setup. Separate required parts, optional upgrades and items the shopper already owns. Generate the smallest feasible basket with live totals and explicit approval. Compare with Constructor-style multi-item recommendations or the merchant's best existing kit. Measure purchasing customers and missing-component failures, not additional lines or order value. Cross-seller execution comes later and requires approved integrations.

6. **I06: recover a correctable checkout error.** Begin with one observed legacy error that is currently classified too broadly or loses valid checkout state. Map it to the negotiated protocol's correct field and recoverable state, then provide the existing continuation route. Compare two valid adapters and retain all sessions assigned before the error. Keep security and commercial rules unchanged. I21 separately restores documented inputs before a risk decision; I35 reconciles an unknown final transaction state.

7. **I07: show the payable total early.** Wrap the merchant's authoritative quote service for one destination-sensitive offer. Include mandatory charges, assumptions and expiry, then compare the quote with the final cart. Test early presentation against an already lawful, accurate native flow at the same prices. Extra orders must survive returns and the full cost ledger. A higher click rate obtained by showing an incomplete headline price is a failure.

8. **I08: ask one decision-changing question.** Use a small merchant-reviewed decision graph to decide whether a missing answer changes the viable shortlist. Show provisional options, allow skip and stop asking once the distinction no longer matters. Compare with the current tuned agent and a short fixed quiz using identical product evidence. Persist assignment across turns and count purchasers, including people who refuse the question. More answered questions alone provides no commercial evidence.

For an initial merchant, I01, I02 and I08 offer relatively bounded owned-store tests. Choose among them using observed eligibility and defects; do not ship all three and attribute the combined result to each. The other five become preferable when the merchant already has the necessary operational interfaces and an important measured failure. Implement the minimum assignment and order ledger alongside the intervention. A trained simulation selector can wait until real trials exist.

Several later bets require especially strong incumbent comparisons. I27 is restricted to a demonstrated wrong-model, wrong-region or stale-stock failure after competent retailer routing. PriceSpider's current destination redirects to Wayvia, which describes retailer availability and purchase paths; its tracking documentation includes retailer sales and basket behavior, subject to activated tracking and partnerships. MikMak also advertises in-stock routing and authorized-retailer integrations. A generic where-to-buy module therefore has no supportable new edge. The proposed final partner recheck must improve brand-wide purchasers using the same partner network. [Wayvia](https://wayvia.com/shoppable-media), [PriceSpider tracking](https://www.pricespider.com/blog/how-pricespiders-universal-tracking-suite-works/), [MikMak partnerships](https://www.mikmak.com/partnership).

I37 is similarly restricted to an actual legacy adapter defect. The current UCP checkout contract already specifies quantity units, scale, increments, sale basis, authoritative totals, visible conversion and recoverable mismatches. It forbids silent reinterpretation. Those are baseline obligations, not our invention. Test a merchant-specific legacy mapping against a correct native or ACS adapter, and use a permitted merchant handoff for an unrepresentable case. [UCP checkout specification](https://ucp.dev/specification/shopping/checkout/).

Physical and operational bets remain in the portfolio because they can change what is genuinely purchasable. Examples include diagnostic samples, verified alterations, real repair access, functioning refill coverage, new assortment pilots and batch-backed usable-life promises. Their dependencies are substantial: sample fulfilment, measurements, service contracts, working capital or batch allocation. An attractive interface cannot supply these capabilities. Each needs its own buyer and unit economics, and none receives an invented uplift range.

Forty reserves preserve useful alternatives without inflating the selected claim. Replenishment reminders and owned-product records mainly target repeat purchases. Rental interval matching targets paid bookings. Delayed-order substitution protects an order already placed. Honest advice to keep an adequate product may benefit later trust, but its incremental-purchase payoff is unestablished. Cost-adaptive model routing stays in reserve until it preserves purchase outcomes against a strong existing router. Protecting authored catalog edits is rejected only as a standalone purchase product; it remains sound implementation practice.

The shared conversation's illustrative percentages do not enter any forecast. Repeating one synthetic shopper does not create independent human buyers, and artificially changing result order does not establish a deployable ranking gain. Publication acceptance is not observed engine exposure. The separate [value and power analysis](value-and-power.md) supplies planning formulas and explicit assumptions; it does not supply expected lift or measured willingness to pay.

Before a live pilot, record eligible AI-origin volume, the failure rate, the available randomization unit, the primary purchase horizon, return or fraud maturation and the minimum commercially useful difference. Public catalog changes need a design that accounts for shared exposure, propagation and substitute products. Low traffic may prevent a useful test within the selling window. In that case, pool only genuinely comparable, permissioned trials or retain uncertainty. Neither simulation repetitions nor a desired commercial price can manufacture the missing purchase evidence.
