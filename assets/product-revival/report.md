# Product revival and creative testing

Research checked 2 October 2026. All proposed prices, operating caps and screening rules are choices to test, not performance forecasts.

Reviving a demonstrated product for a better-defined buyer is worth testing. The evidence supports a lean product-and-creative pipeline; it does not establish a current profitable winner for our store.
Use twelve candidates for desk research, narrow to two for exact quotes, then fund one sample and one active offer only when the costs fit. Multiple paid shops would spread the same small budget across setup, stock and support before we know which buying reason works.
The strongest immediate research shortlist is a heatless-curl comfort kit, followed by an independently designed adjustable sleep mask. Actual sample selection still needs a complete delivered quote and quality check. This is a judgement about testing practicality, not verified demand or profit.
A new background is useful when it helps the right buyer recognise the use case. Copy a clear demonstration structure and reshoot it with a sample, or obtain an explicit commercial editing licence. Competitor clips, logos, customer reviews and a false ownership prompt are not assets we can assume we own.
Organic posts can screen openings and objections without ad spend. They cannot establish a causal background effect or scalable paid acquisition. Track relevant visits, the all-in price, checkout, fulfilled orders, refunds and money left after costs separately.
The current preparation records still show zero paid orders and £0 recorded cash spending. This research made no sample purchase, paid activation, social post or new account. The existing £200 shared conditional pilot ceiling and £75 protected reserve are unchanged.

## The first controlled workload

Choose one buyer and one physical sample. Film a truthful complete demonstration. Screen three openings across the untouched setting and one buyer-relevant alternative, six clips in total. Make two fresh-take repeats of the most promising cut. This eight-post cap is our workload rule, not a TikTok requirement or a proven minimum sample size. Snapshot each at 72 hours and seven days; record reach and geography, retention, qualified clicks, landing sessions and price-visible buying interest. Do not compare unrelated products by view count. Freeze the offer, product, duration and CTA while testing one factor.

Treat organic differences as directional. Keep insufficient exposure separate from exposed indifference. After the predeclared batch and one evidence-led correction, pause when no useful buying signal emerges. A paid comparison needs exclusive randomised groups, one variable and adequate power. Native TikTok budget minimums may not fit this pilot; a single promoted post is a directional acquisition check, not a causal A/B test.

No platform fee, sample purchase, creator gift, AI subscription or advertising was paid for this report.
