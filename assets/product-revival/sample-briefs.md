# Two products to qualify before opening an offer

Prepared 2 October 2026. These are quote and inspection briefs. Neither product has been sourced, bought or accepted. Proposed prices are research choices. Select at most one physical sample if its complete delivered charge fits the existing £25 validation allowance and the saved funding conditions.

## Heatless curl comfort kit

The proposed buyer has medium-to-long hair, wants less morning styling work and found an ordinary rigid rod uncomfortable. Check whether that problem matters enough to pay for a better kit. A bathrobe belt is a real competing method.

Ask for an exact unbranded variant with a soft rod, two secure ties, clip if needed, washable pouch and material/care specification. Ask separately about cotton filling and foam. Satin describes a weave; it does not establish silk fibre. No brand, silk percentage or certificate claim transfers from the photographed reference product.

The quote must identify one sample, its contents, GBP delivered total, applicable taxes, shipping method, arrival estimate, repeat-order price, quantity/size/colour requirements, packaging dimensions and replacement terms. The public US$1.37-US$2.63 listing has MOQ 10. It is not a one-unit delivered quote or evidence of SILKE-equivalent quality.

Inspect seams, exposed hard parts, tie tension, dye transfer, care and the packed format. Record one complete overnight trial: hair type and length, starting moisture, technique, application time, side-sleep comfort, morning result and later hold. Show an unsuccessful attempt when it changes the instructions. Broader hair-type claims need broader trials.

Three draft messages, before any customer availability claim:

- Morning preparation: show the real morning result and how much wrapping work preceded it.
- Overnight comfort: show the rod against a pillow and explain the actual tester's experience.
- Technique: show placement, tension and moisture that worked for the tested hair, without promising the same result to everyone.

The proposed £39-£49 basket still needs a complete cost model and price-visible UK buying interest. Do not add accessories merely to justify the price. Each addition must solve a stated problem and leave enough contribution.

## Independently designed contoured sleep mask

The proposed buyer is bothered by daylight leaking around the nose or by pressure when lying on a pillow. Night-shift rest and travel are separate occasions to screen. Confirm who has the problem before assuming the curl-kit audience also wants this.

Ask for an independent design with sufficient lash space, a comfortable adjustable strap, washable materials and an exact specification. Do not request a clone of Manta's protected construction or copy its performance statements. A £29 Manta Original is a competing UK offer; the £69 PRO is a premium reference, not proof of our price acceptance.

The indexed generic supplier lists JPY450 per unit at quantity 2-199 and JPY804 for a sample. Logo MOQ is 200; packaging MOQ is 500. Those old indexed terms have no confirmed current UK delivery charge. Get the same complete delivered quote fields as above and distinguish one sample from a production MOQ.

Inspect nose leakage in ordinary daylight, strap adjustment, lash clearance, pillow pressure, edge friction, odour, seam finish, wash recovery and colour transfer. Record head/face fit limitations. A staged darkness camera exposure is not an honest blackout test. Do not claim treatment of insomnia or other medical conditions.

Three draft messages:

- Daylight gap: show fit around the nose under consistent lighting.
- Side pressure: show the actual profile against a pillow, then the tester's own comfort account.
- Adjustment: show how the same mask fits the actual tester before and after adjusting its strap.

The proposed £39-£49 offer needs a measurable reason to choose it over cheaper masks. Packaging cannot substitute for comfort or fit.

## The first filming workload

After one sample passes, choose one demonstration and one offer. Film three openings in the untouched setting and one relevant alternative setting: six variants. Add two fresh takes of the promising cut, giving eight posts in total. This is a workload cap chosen for this pilot, not a platform rule or statistical sample-size claim.

Test the opening before changing the setting. Register each changed factor separately; hold product, demonstration, length, offer and CTA fixed wherever that is the question. Record snapshots at 72 hours and seven days. Include failed posts, relevant geography, watch measures, qualified destination clicks, price-visible landing sessions, concrete objections and production time. Different algorithmic audiences make this directional screening. It cannot establish causal background lift or paid CAC.

No single delivered sample price is confirmed. Actual sample quality, complete variable costs, accepted footage and current new-brand demand remain unknown. Those are the next evidence gaps. Do not replace them with a large minimum order, a custom box order or a second paid store.

The shared conditional £200 ceiling remains £50 platform, £25 validation, £10 payment checks, £40 traffic and £75 protected reserve. The chosen product screen remains £25 before acquisition and £15 after measured acquisition, after other variable costs and servicing time but before fixed costs. Neither candidate has passed it.
