Your order assumptions

Illustrative UK cash model. GBP. Non-VAT-registered business.

Supplier costs include irrecoverable taxes. The model excludes founder labour. These figures are assumptions, not expected profit.

Use the total customer payment, including any shipping charged. Enter the actual outbound shipping cost separately.

The returns reserve is the average cash loss allowed for refunds, return handling and unrecovered costs across all orders.

Acquisition is cash spent to win one order. Zero cash spend still leaves the founder's time outside this model.

The default £30 is a planning allowance: the published £25 Shopify price plus £5 tax headroom. It is not a confirmed tax-inclusive invoice. The break-even result covers only the fixed costs entered here.

How this is calculated

Contribution per order = customer payment − production and decoration − outbound shipping − payment fees − returns reserve − acquisition cash.

Payment fees = customer payment × the percentage fee + the fixed fee. Orders to cover monthly fixed costs = monthly fixed costs ÷ positive contribution per order, rounded up. A zero or negative contribution cannot cover fixed costs in this model.

This is a per-order cash contribution model. Founder labour, taxes on business income, upfront development costs and any costs omitted from your inputs still need funding.
