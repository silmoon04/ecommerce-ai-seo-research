# Quiet fandom clothing: three art directions

Research and concept proposal, 29 September 2026. The route titles below are working labels, with no claim that a brand name has been cleared.

The strongest starting point is Second passage: compact route marks, convincing everyday silhouettes, and a dark palette that suggests persistence without borrowing a game's symbols. Held frame offers a cleaner anime connection through motion. Mending hour brings warmth and a more illustrative option. Select one visual language for the first capsule; mixing all three would make a small launch look unresolved.

**What the references establish.** [Sunspel's heavyweight tee](https://us.sunspel.com/products/mens-relaxed-fit-heavyweight-tshirt-in-black-mtsh0181-bkaa) presents a plain silhouette, substantial crew collar, Supima cotton and a named brushing finish. The useful lesson is to make fit and material decisions visible. [Goldwin x Cactus Store](https://usshop.goldwin-global.com/products/gl56532cc?variant=42713984499774) places a flower on one lower pocket of an otherwise restrained shirt. Its principle is a memorable detail with generous empty fabric around it. [Story mfg.'s Grateful Tee](https://www.storymfg.com/collections/story-mfg-1/products/grateful-tee-ss-charcoal-take-a-hike) names the illustrator, printing and embroidery, and explains colour variation. Borrow that specificity when supported by production, while keeping our graphics small. These are design references, not proposed blanks or suppliers. Sunspel and Story product views and Goldwin's official garment image were visually inspected.

**A common garment foundation.** Use two blank types: a relaxed, regular-length tee and a relaxed crew sweatshirt. Sampling targets are 230–260 GSM cotton jersey and 320–360 GSM loopback respectively, subject to comfort and supplier availability. Seek a moderate shoulder drop, substantial rib collar, neat shoulder taping and straight hems. Weight alone cannot establish quality. The first capsule has three designs, two on the same tee and one on the crew, each initially offered in one colour. Keep sleeve and seam decoration out of this first production brief. Specify the artwork in millimetres and approve placement against the selected supplier's templates.

**Second passage: weathered routefinding.** Its story is returning to a difficult place with more knowledge. The mark consists of two squared U-shaped paths, offset and open on opposite sides, with one deliberate break. Repetition carries the meaning. Keep the drawing asymmetric enough to avoid an occult-looking seal. No swords, bonfires, rings, copied runes or distressed medieval type.

Use washed charcoal `#30312F`, slate `#636B69`, bone `#DCD6C9` and a small clay accent `#9D6650`. These are screen references; approve physical colours separately. A narrow, plain grotesk with restrained spacing suits labels and website copy. Garments need no exterior slogan.

Use flat satin embroidery for the stout paths, with clear open spaces. The quieter route reads as clothing first; the story becomes apparent at close range. Its risk is looking like generic hiking apparel. Show the same path evolving across all three products, and explain the idea through one concise product sentence about returning and learning.

**Held frame: motion held still.** Translate animation timing into three displaced open rectangular corners. Repeat one shape with controlled offsets; the gap between positions does the work. Avoid faces, manga panels, random speed lines and pseudo-Japanese lettering. Each release can vary the sequence while preserving the same angles and proportions.

The palette is midnight `#242D3E`, chalk `#E7E5DC`, blue-grey `#8998A8` and a single dusty violet `#9B8DA5`. Use a compact sans for product names and occasional monospaced numbers in the website's editorial captions, never ornamental technical gibberish.

It has the strongest graphic identity, but animation enthusiasts may read motion rather than a specific fandom. That is the intended level of recognition.

**Mending hour: recovery and repair.** This is the warmest route. Two rounded blocks separated by a gap are joined with two stout bridge stitches. One small original drawing of a repaired cloth pouch supplies an alternative to pure geometry. Rest, useful objects and visible mending connect the collection to quiet intervals between adventures.

Use oat `#DED3BF`, faded moss `#747C64`, warm charcoal `#494540` and clay `#AA7158`. A sturdy serif for editorial titles and a simple sans for practical information give the identity a bookish tone. Avoid faux handwritten authenticity claims.

Keep the other face blank. The decorative bridges should look deliberately drawn and stitched; they do not establish that the garment was hand-repaired. Mending hour has the weakest immediate gaming signal, but may attract people who want a gentler wardrobe. Test the pouch illustration against the abstract mark before choosing the lead image.

**Production checks.** Start embroidery drawings with 1.5 mm strokes, gaps of at least 6.5 mm and no tiny lettering. Printful publishes flat-embroidery minimums of about 1.27 mm line thickness and 6.35 mm for text height and negative space. Other providers may differ; confirm digitisation and placement with the chosen decorator. ([Printful guidance](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50263985175953-How-should-I-prepare-my-design-for-embroidery)) For print trials, propose solid vector shapes and strokes of at least 0.75 mm, then obtain the printer's limits. Those print dimensions are our starting specification, not an industry guarantee.

Sample the exact garment, colour, decoration and size combination. Record finished measurements before and after five label-compliant washes; inspect collar recovery, twisting, puckering, backing against skin and print wear. Photograph front, back, worn fit and detail. Generated boards establish mood and proportions only; they cannot verify fibre, hand feel or durability.

**A bounded visual test.** Build six controlled alternatives for each route: original object drawing, abstract motif and plain-word treatment, each at two sizes. Keep blank, camera, lighting and colour constant. Randomise their order for a small pilot including anime fans, Soulslike players and people outside either audience. First ask which they would wear to a café or dinner; then ask what the mark suggests without revealing the story. Record recognition separately from purchase preference. Compare the three products together with names hidden to test whether they look related. At actual print size, reject any version that loses its gaps or depends on a macro crop to become legible.
