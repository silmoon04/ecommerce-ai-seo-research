# Product photography references

These eight images come from the brands' or supplier's product pages. They are third-party product photographs, separate from our AI-generated design concepts. The six competitor images support visual comparison; the two blank images support garment selection. They are references, not photographs of our products.

The source pages and exact image URLs were identified during the preceding research. On 29 September 2026, all eight image URLs returned HTTP 200 to HEAD requests with an image content type. No image files were downloaded. This check confirms URL availability; visual inspection remains part of the report review.

The machine-readable manifest is [visual-references.json](visual-references.json). Every item uses `kind: "reference"`; `category` distinguishes competitor references from supplier blanks. Prices, specifications and sourcing judgments belong in [competitors.md](competitors.md) and [sourcing.md](sourcing.md), rather than being inferred from these photographs.

Two targeted source checks followed the browser review. The TORCH TORCH image now uses the main product image explicitly present in the official page's HTML (`600__0000_1_Memsis_Cage_Brack.jpg`), replacing its 150-pixel thumbnail. The [current IMOURI product data](https://imouri.com/products/oc-t-shirt.js) confirms that `IMOURIOCTSHIRT1.jpg?v=1759467045` is the OC T-Shirt's featured image. Its description specifies front embroidery measuring 10 inches across in three colours. This is a bolder reference for motif scale; the large lettering is consistent with the current product rather than evidence of an incorrect photo.
