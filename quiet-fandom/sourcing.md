# Sourcing and sampling notes

Checked 29 September 2026. UK purchasing and initial UK customers are assumptions, not confirmed business facts. Prices below are supplier charges, not proposed customer retail prices. No accounts, quotations, purchases or physical tests were completed.

Start with one tee and, if the budget allows, one sweatshirt or hoodie. Inkthreadable is the strongest first sampling route because its UK prices include a named blank and decoration. Choose Freestyler if its shape and textured handle beat the cheaper Creator in wearing tests. A small embroidered original motif suits the understated brief. Custom cut-and-sew adds development and production commitments before demand is established; defer it.

These five candidates answer different questions. Sample dark solid colours first; heather compositions can differ. GSM describes fabric mass per square metre, not softness, stitching quality, shrinkage or durability.

Inkthreadable has no online minimum. The price includes **one available print or embroidery position**. Extra positions cost £5.04 including VAT; a printed tee neck label adds £1.51. Printed labels generally cannot be added to its hoodies or sweatshirts. Standard embroidery allows 10 × 10 cm, 12,000 stitches and minimum 1.25 mm lines. Try a 35–55 mm motif; unusual cuff or hem positions require confirmation. Digitisation guidelines list £12.60/design without stating VAT basis; the FAQ lists £10.50 plus VAT for bulk embroidery but describes standard orders as having no setup fees. Budget provisionally for £12.60 and check the configured order. For batches, AS Colour's published Heavy Tee prices are **retail quantity discounts**, not wholesale quotes. Wholesale requires approval and £250 monthly spend to retain pricing; decorated resale is required. Stanley/Stella also requires an approved partner/decorator route, and no public UK blank price was verified. Its no-garment-minimum policy does not remove a printer's own MOQ. [AS Colour account terms](https://ascolour.co.uk/wholesale-faqs), [Stanley/Stella sourcing](https://stanleystella.com/en-gb/bu/bu-b2b-info), [B2B terms](https://stanleystella.com/en-eu/terms-conditions).

Two practical UK batch benchmarks are [Blackwater Studios](https://blackwaterstudios.co.uk/faq/), whose FAQ gives 25-piece minimums for one/two-colour screen printing and ten for embroidery, and [Xcel in Haverhill](https://xceluk.com/customisation-pricing/), which lists small embroidery up to roughly 5,000 stitches at £3.10 excluding VAT for 25–49 pieces or £2.65 for 50–99, excluding garments; digitisation is free. These are not combined AS Colour quotes. Supplied-blank acceptance, spoilage responsibility and delivery need agreement. At 50 units, Heavy Tee blanks plus Xcel's listed embroidery alone would be £18.78 including VAT per garment **if those separate terms were accepted**, before labels, packing and postage. That illustrates why batch is not automatically cheaper. Inkthreadable also offers quotation-only batches from 25 of one design. Test before photographing or selling. Record garment code, size, colour, provider, method, thread colour, artwork dimensions, cost and dispatch time. Measure chest, length, sleeve and neck opening; photograph fit on different bodies. Keep an unwashed control. Wash inside-out at 30°C and line dry, following the decorated garment's instructions; measure after one, five and ten washes. Assess shrinkage, seam twist, collar recovery, pilling, fading, print cracking and embroidery puckering/backing comfort. These are proposed internal checks, not laboratory certification.

Reject visible peeling, broken stitches, irritating backing or a fit that becomes unacceptable after washing. Set dimensional tolerances before reviewing results; a provisional maximum 5% change can be a decision rule, not a supplier guarantee. Reorder the winning sample separately to check repeatability. Then choose one tee and one warm layer, with one decoration position each. Keep scarce launch cash for replenishment, replacements and size exchanges rather than extra garment styles.
