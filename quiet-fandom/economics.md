# Economics of a small original clothing label

Research checked 29 September 2026. GBP throughout. A UK-established seller, UK fulfilment, UK customers, one garment per order and no VAT registration are working assumptions, not confirmed user facts. These are scenarios, not revenue forecasts.

A small POD launch can test the product without buying a size run. The harder question is whether the margin pays for finding customers and the founder's time. The model below uses a substantial tee and hoodie rather than the cheapest catalogue garments. Fabric weight still needs a wear-and-wash test.

Inkthreadable lists its 240 gsm Freestyler tee at £19.69 including VAT and its 350 gsm Cruiser 2.0 hoodie at £36.42. One decoration location is included; an extra location costs £5.04. A printed tee neck label adds £1.51. Hoodies cannot use this printed-label treatment, so the hoodie model retains the supplier's standard labelling. Check the chosen size, colour and artwork in a final quote. [Catalogue](https://www.inkthreadable.co.uk/organic-products), [pricing](https://help.inkthreadable.co.uk/en/articles/15452531-product-pricing-reward-tiers), [relabelling](https://help.inkthreadable.co.uk/en/articles/455795-relabelling-your-garments).

POD shipping is £2.88 per tee and £3.78 per hoodie using the supplier's assigned shipping weights and Royal Mail 48 service. These are supplier charging weights, not measured garment weights. [Shipping schedule](https://www.inkthreadable.co.uk/shipping-costs).

The payment input is Shopify Basic's published UK standard online card rate of 2% plus 25p, applied to the full checkout amount. Basic is £25 monthly or £19/month billed yearly. Other cards and methods can cost more. The launch budgets allow £30/month, comprising £25 plus £5 tax headroom, rather than relying on promotions. [Shopify pricing](https://www.shopify.com/uk/pricing).

The reserve assumes 8% of orders receive a full product-and-delivery refund, plus separate replacement fulfilment on 2% of orders. Both rates are modelling choices, not measured apparel averages. Returned-stock recovery and supplier reimbursements are zero in this calculation. It assumes customers pay non-fault return postage under clearly disclosed terms; add merchant-funded labels if offered. Shopify keeps original processing fees on refunded orders. Ordinary online cancellations can require refunding standard delivery. [Shopify refunds](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/payouts/refunds), [GOV.UK returns](https://www.gov.uk/accepting-returns-and-giving-refunds).

Formula: contribution = 0.92 × checkout receipts − 1.02 × fulfilment cost − payment fee. The reserve funds expected losses; it is not a promise that every bad batch is covered. At a 15% refund assumption, tee contribution falls to £10.01 before labour and acquisition.

For an unregistered seller, supplier VAT remains a cost and sales receipts are not divided by 1.2. The UK registration threshold is taxable turnover above £90,000 over the previous 12 months, or expected above £90,000 in the next 30 days. Adult clothing is modelled at the standard 20% rate. Once registered, keeping the same consumer prices and recovering eligible input VAT reduces these contributions to approximately £10.60 and £22.29 before labour and acquisition. Recalculate from valid invoices. [Registration](https://www.gov.uk/register-for-vat), [VAT rates](https://www.gov.uk/vat-rates), [input VAT](https://www.gov.uk/charge-reclaim-record-vat/reclaim-vat-business-expenses).

An extra printed location reduces tee contribution to £7.80. Free delivery at the same £38 garment price leaves £9.39. These choices need to earn their cost through a price increase, more orders or fewer returns.

The batch comparison uses **quote ceilings**, not observed decorated-garment prices: £17 per tee and £31 per hoodie, including decoration and agreed branding. Add assumed inbound allocation of £0.50, packaging of £0.50, and £3.65 outward postage. Royal Mail's online small-parcel Tracked 48 price is £3.65 including VAT; dimensions and drop-off must qualify. Contribution becomes £15.42/£31.74, or £12.42/£28.74 after 12 minutes' labour. [Royal Mail](https://www.royalmail.com/sending/uk/tracked-48).

Those ceilings may be unattainable at the chosen quality. AS Colour's public 50-plus price for the 280 gsm Heavy Tee is already £15.60 including VAT before decoration. At £20 decorated production cost, batch tee contribution falls to £12.36 before labour. Buy stock for proven demand, quality control or dispatch speed; a small batch does not establish a margin improvement. [AS Colour Heavy Tee](https://ascolour.co.uk/heavy-tee-5080/).

POD seeding buys three delivered tees. Batch stock is 50 × £17, £60 setup, £25 inbound and £25 mailers. Three garments are gifts, leaving 47 for customers; the photo/seeding line is £50 plus three £3.65 deliveries. Preorder photos/seeding are £50 plus three POD tees. Its float includes £75 for payment fees if a campaign is refunded. Floats and contingency are available cash, not immediate expenses. Cap simultaneous supplier commitments to cash actually available.

A legitimate preorder needs a tested sample, accepted supplier quote, clear closing date, minimum quantity and delivery date, and a refund plan if the threshold fails. GOV.UK requires delivery within 30 days unless another timing is agreed. For 50 tee preorders under the assumptions above, £2,097.50 collected leaves £711.10 after production, setup, inbound/outbound delivery, packaging, processing and the reserves. That remainder must cover campaign overhead and work. Keep customer receipts available until the manufacturing decision; do not treat the entire collection as profit. [Online selling rules](https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses/online-selling).

Acquisition cost includes adverts, gifted stock, postage and paid content. Three gifted tees cost £72.24: five resulting customers would mean £14.45 each before commissions. A proposed 10% creator commission on retained garment receipts, excluding shipping, costs £3.50 per tee in this model. Video-reuse permission has no verified price here. Enter its agreed fee as a fixed cost and record the permitted usage before spending; do not assume the existing video is free advertising.

The POD cash expenses are £297.24 after separating float and contingency. Cash break-even requires 23 tee orders or 11 hoodie orders at the model margins. Now value 60 setup hours plus four hours/week for 12 weeks at £15/hour: fixed labour adds £1,620. After the per-order labour above, economic break-even becomes 184 tees or 78 hoodies. At an assumed £8 acquisition cost, it becomes 785 tees or 116 hoodies. These are separate single-product arithmetic cases, not sales expectations.

General formula: orders required = ceiling((fixed cash expenses + fixed founder labour) ÷ (contribution − acquisition cost − variable founder labour)). A zero or negative denominator cannot pay back the launch. Taxes on profit, larger support workloads and further operating months would raise the requirement. The immediate decision is to validate willingness to pay and the samples before committing to a batch or buying reach.
