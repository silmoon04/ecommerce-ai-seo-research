# Monitoring platforms: OtterlyAI and Peec AI

**Snapshot: 2026-09-29.** These vendors sell observations about AI answers; “visibility,” citations, ranking and referral attribution are distinct from incremental, profitable sales. Neither reviewed disclosure establishes actual company profit, margins, cash flow, or customer ROI generally. Prices below are vendor-published USD monthly prices unless stated otherwise; plan pages can change.

## OtterlyAI

### The product and what it does well

OtterlyAI wraps prompt monitoring in a marketer-friendly workflow: discover prompts, track brand and competitor mentions, inspect citations and rankings, view a brand visibility index/domain rankings, run technical GEO audits, and receive recommendations. Its live pricing lists Lite at $29 for 15 prompts and Standard at $189 for 100, Premium at $489 for 400, and custom Enterprise beginning at 1,000; annual prices are $25/$160/$422 per month. Core plans include ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Copilot. Claude, Google AI Mode and Gemini are add-ons, individually priced by tier; Standard/Premium extra capacity is $99 per 100 prompts monthly. Daily checks are now standard. Lite includes three recommendations weekly; paid levels raise URL audit, API, MCP and agent analytics quotas. Standard/Premium list 2,000/5,000 API and MCP requests and 200k/1M agent events monthly. ([Pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing), [monitoring cadence](https://help.otterly.ai/monitoring-interval))

This packaging gives small teams an entry point and larger accounts reasons to upgrade. It supports 50+ countries, exports, and agent analytics, which ingests server logs to show bot visits. That should remain distinct from answer mentions. Daily runs avoid stale snapshots, but do not run at a fixed hour and cannot be manually triggered or tuned. ([Pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing), [cadence details](https://help.otterly.ai/monitoring-interval))

### Limits and profit pressure

For an ecommerce operator, brand-level prompts can miss SKU selection, variant correctness, price/availability accuracy, and whether a recommendation sends a buyer to the right product. Otterly has GEO URL audits and recommendations, but the public plan description does not show a Shopify catalog connection or a SKU-level shopping analytics loop; treat that as a documented-scope limit, not proof that no custom service exists. Daily monitoring also means repeated outputs from nondeterministic services are samples, not a universal rank guarantee. The vendor itself says answers can vary and daily observations accumulate signal. A merchant still has to decide which prompt matters, interpret the cited source, author a safe fix, publish it, wait for recrawl, and validate. That human research and change-management work can dominate a small account’s subscription value. ([Cadence article](https://help.otterly.ai/monitoring-interval), [pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing))

Vendor COGS/query cost are not public. At 15 prompts × four default engines × 30 days, Lite entails ~1,800 answer observations/month; 100 prompts ~12,000; 400 prompts ~48,000, before add-ons/retries. This is volume arithmetic, not actual API calls or COGS: caching, browser automation, paid access and support change unit economics. Neither margins nor public ARR/funding/profitability were verified; product pricing cannot establish profit.

### What to improve / ecommerce offer

Build a compact merchant path: import Shopify catalog, select a few high-intent shopper questions per product category, show answer/product/citation with timestamp, and map a detected mismatch to the exact field and market. Label sampled answers, confidence, geography and product eligibility; distinguish mention from referral/session/order and from causal lift. Provide a suggested, sourced change with merchant approval, publish/rollback history and recheck. Start with a weekly, capped validation cohort and reserve daily tracking for volatile/high-value queries. Offer a fixed-price “catalog answerability and evidence” setup plus recurring checks; charge by active SKU cohort/markets and observation budget, not an opaque universal visibility score. Test willingness to pay and operator minutes per store before promising margin. Borrow Otterly’s straightforward tiers, trial, country coverage, recommendation/export workflow and explicit operational cadence. Do not clone branding, UI, labels, scoring, or proprietary collection methods. Don’t promise rank, sales lift, causal attribution from last-click referrals, or that a crawler audit alone unlocks shopping placement.

No independently attributable Otterly buyer review was included in the reviewed primary-source set; this does not mean none exist. “Trusted by” logos are not merchant outcome evidence.

## Peec AI

### Product, price and reported business evidence

Peec began with brand/agency prompt analytics and has expanded toward a connected measurement stack. Brand plans publicly enumerate Starter (50 prompts, three models, one project), Pro (150, three models, two projects), Advanced (350, three models, five projects, multi-country and Looker Studio), and custom Enterprise with API, SSO and up to 13 models; annual billing is said to save 15%. The page’s crawler-readable copy did not expose the numeric plan cards on this snapshot, while a G2 listing and Peec-authored pricing instructions state $95/$245/$495 monthly. Use that price series as a sourced reported quote, but recheck the checkout page before modeling current price. March 2026 company-authored pricing update says plans were expanded and made more flexible, with project-level model/frequency allocation and free seven-day pitch projects. It explicitly framed this as lowering short-term revenue per prompt, not evidence of current losses or profitability. ([Pricing](https://peec.ai/pricing), [company pricing update](https://peec.ai/blog/pricing-update), [G2 listing](https://www.g2.com/products/peec-ai/reviews))

Peec reported growth in ARR across two dated disclosures: its Nov. 18, 2025 announcement reports $4M+ ARR and $29M total funding; its May 28, 2026 release reports $10M ARR and the same funding. The later release is the latest company-reported ARR, not audited revenue or profit. Neither discloses margin, cash burn or net income, so profitability remains unknown. ([Series A announcement](https://peec.ai/blog/we-raised-21m-series-a-to-help-brands-win-in-ai-search), [May 2026 ARR announcement](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/28/3303009/0/en/Peec-AI-hits-10M-ARR-16-months-after-launch-as-brands-race-to-optimize-for-AI-search.html))

### What it does well; fit and friction

The plan page lists AI Shopping: Shopify/CSV/Google Merchant Center catalog input, SKU visibility/position/win rate, product query fanouts and retrieved source URLs. The June 17 launch describes a public Shopify feed/CSV setup and product facts, price, variants and merchant competition. September AI Referrals joins GA4 sessions, engagement, conversions and revenue by assistant/page/country/device. Its “My website” requires GA4 plus server/CDN logs to connect crawls, retrieval and visits. Peec warns AI Mode/Overviews can be classed as organic and mobile/no-referrer visits as direct. ([AI Shopping launch](https://peec.ai/blog/ai-shopping-analytics), [pricing feature list](https://peec.ai/pricing), [AI Referrals](https://peec.ai/blog/introducing-ai-referrals))

Peec defines one answer as one prompt × one active model × one run. Starter’s 50 × 3 × daily × ~30 is 4,500 answers/month; Pro ~13,500 and Advanced ~31,500. This is usage volume, not actual provider calls or COGS: retries, crawling, parsing, onboarding and support may add costs; caching may reduce calls. Self-serve is daily; Enterprise can be daily/weekly. No unit cost or actual profit evidence is public. ([Pricing and coverage](https://peec.ai/pricing))

G2 showed 11 reviews at 4.9/5 when indexed. March–April 2026 reviewers praised interface, setup and support; some also cited manual exports/filtering, source extraction, extra-model cost or need for onboarding. This small self-selected sample is not ecommerce ROI evidence. Peec’s Mint Position case study claims 16% ChatGPT visibility lift in 90 days for Duckfund and 20% agency YoY growth; a single $500K prospect-deal anecdote does not establish Peec-caused or ecommerce ROI. ([G2 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/peec-ai/reviews), [Mint Position case study](https://peec.ai/blog/how-mint-position-helps-fintech-clients-unlock-16-more-ai-search-visibility-and-secure-1-search-positions-with-peec-ai))

### Opportunity and product response

Its SKU analytics narrow the whitespace: specialize in product-fact provenance and safe corrections. Identify conflicting variant attributes across Shopify and feeds, link them to the query/market evidence, distinguish eligibility, retrieval, recommendation and order, then offer an approved cited edit and propagation check. Keep referral attribution separate from holdout testing. CSV and read-only Shopify should deliver an initial diagnosis without mandatory CDN logs or GA4.

Borrow answer-volume math, flexible cadence, source URLs, SKU detail and crawl-to-referral measurement. Reduce manual exports with prioritized actions and reversible field edits. Pilot a catalog cohort with query pack, evidence-linked fixes and 30-day recheck; price by complexity and operator time. Test conversion, holdout contribution and renewal after cleanup. Do not claim Peec is unprofitable or copy its identity, scores or data pipeline.
