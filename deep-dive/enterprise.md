# Enterprise monitoring platforms: Profound and Scrunch

**Snapshot:** 2026-09-29. Pricing and capabilities below were checked against current first-party vendor pages and documentation. Vendor customer counts, performance claims, and positioning are company claims unless identified otherwise. Neither company publishes audited standalone profitability, gross margin, CAC, churn, or unit economics in the sources reviewed; funding, valuation, customer count, and parent-company finances do not establish profitability.

## Profound

Profound is a broad enterprise AI search marketing suite: answer-engine visibility measurement, prompt-volume research, shopping insights, referral/agent analytics, and a workflow builder that can turn findings into content and publishing actions. It is aimed at brand and agency teams that need multi-engine, multi-market reporting and operational execution, not a small merchant seeking a one-click Shopify SEO fix. On September 15, Profound announced a $180M Series D at a $1.8B valuation, bringing disclosed cumulative funding to at least $335M after its earlier $155M+ disclosure. The same release claims more than 1,000 enterprise brands and over one third of the Fortune 100. These are company disclosures; the announcement gives no ARR or profitability figure. [Series D announcement](https://www.tryprofound.com/newsroom/profound-raises-usd180m-series-d-at-usd1-8b-valuation-to-build-the-ai-platform-for-marketing-teams)

Pricing differs by audience and the vendor appears to be transitioning its public offer. The current Brands tab on the live pricing page presents a free seven-day trial—50 prompts daily across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews, limited AI Marketer credits, and a recommended prompt set that cannot be customized—and custom Enterprise for ongoing tracking. Trial and Enterprise both list unlimited seats; the Enterprise price and prompt limits are negotiated. The page FAQ describes the choice as Trial or Enterprise. Separately, the public help FAQ still describes a self-serve Agency Growth plan with 400 Agent credits per client workspace, while a vendor agency launch post prices each Agency Growth workspace at $399/month. Older vendor pages and search-indexed pricing variants retain Starter $99 and Growth $399 brand tiers; this is evidence of an earlier/alternate plan presentation, not evidence that the current Brands offer contradicts itself or that $399 applies to a brand Growth plan today. [Agency Growth offer](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/agencies-launch-your-aeo-practice-with-profound), [credit FAQ](https://help.tryprofound.com/articles/9359511602-profound-agents-credits). Agent credits are metered by run complexity, with estimated and actual use visible, and accounts can be configured to pause or incur overages. [Pricing](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing)

Collection is a meaningful differentiator, but do not confuse the phrase “consumer experience” with representative consumer behavior. Profound says its responses are collected through consumer-facing interfaces rather than model APIs. Its product documentation says datasets are generated from structured prompts; Enterprise prompt sets can be manually added, uploaded, or generated, and prompts refresh daily on Eastern Time. A new prompt may take 24–48 hours to yield initial visibility. Persona variants increase executions because the base and persona-context prompt run separately. These are repeatable observations of a configured sample, not an unbiased sample of actual shoppers’ private conversations. Results remain sensitive to prompt construction, geography, personalization/session state, product availability and answer-engine changes. An honest report should disclose prompt set, engine, market, run time, repeat count, and whether citation, mention, rank, or attributed traffic is being counted. [Prompt setup](https://help.tryprofound.com/articles/3730240593-create-manage-and-tag-prompts), [prompt guidance](https://help.tryprofound.com/articles/4881350168-how-to-create-topics-prompts-to-track-in-profound)

The expansion from analytics to action is real and potentially valuable for large teams. Agents use a node canvas, can call Profound data, web research, LLMs, sheets/CSV, and publishing integrations; workflows may draft or update CMS content. This shortens handoffs and provides a recurring workflow surface. It also increases product, model, and support costs: agent credits meter complex runs, integration reliability and human review become essential, and generated copy is not evidence of ranking lift. Its official materials report integrations including CMS, cloud, analytics, and CDN providers, while enterprise adds tailored prompt/agent capacity, dedicated support, and SSO/SAML/SOC2. [Agents overview](https://help.tryprofound.com/articles/9762251986-agents-overview)

Likely profit pressures are hypotheses, not reported company costs: expensive front-end collection and multi-engine refreshes; model and data processing for analysis; product breadth and integration maintenance; and high-touch enterprise selling/onboarding/support. Funding and rapid hiring/customer growth may fund these costs but say nothing about whether contribution margins are positive. Improve by publishing stable plan matrices and effective availability dates, making sampling limits and audit trails explicit, offering lower-touch onboarding with self-serve workflows, and measuring agent actions by accepted/published change and downstream outcome rather than run count. For a small-merchant product, borrow the sampled prompt → cited source → prioritized action → verification loop, prompt/credit transparency, and direct workflow integration. Avoid copying enterprise dashboards, vague “visibility” scores, expensive broad engine coverage, and autonomous publishing without approval/rollback. Profound’s strength is breadth and execution for staffed marketing teams; its weakness for small ecommerce is price opacity, breadth overhead, and weak direct connection from catalog-level fix to incremental profitable orders.

## Scrunch

Scrunch has repositioned from AI visibility monitoring into an “Agent Experience Platform” (AXP): observe prompts and on-site agent traffic, diagnose content gaps, and serve AI agents a structured version of web pages. It is an enterprise marketing/web infrastructure product, particularly suited to large brands with JavaScript-heavy sites and a CDN or web team. Scrunch states it has 500+ brands/agencies, raised $26M before being acquired by Sitecore on June 3, 2026, and continues as a standalone platform. Sitecore’s release confirms the acquisition but gives no purchase price or Scrunch financials. Sitecore separately reports more than $500M ARR in Scrunch’s FAQ/About material; that is the parent’s claim, not Scrunch revenue or profitability. No public Scrunch ARR, operating profit, gross margin, CAC, or churn was located. [Scrunch history](https://scrunch.com/about/), [Sitecore acquisition announcement](https://www.sitecore.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/2026/06/sitecore-acquires-scrunch-to-help-brands-influence-discovery--and-buying-decisions)

Scrunch pricing pages have changed substantially across recent captures. The page currently rendered at the public pricing URL lists Starter at $250/month billed annually or $300 month-to-month (3 seats, 350 custom prompts, 1,000 industry prompts, 3 personas, 5 page audits); Growth at $417/month annually or $500 month-to-month (5 seats, 700 custom prompts, 2,500 industry prompts, 5 personas, 10 audits); and custom Enterprise. It lists seven-day Starter trial, Google SSO in all plans, customer success and a 30-minute onboarding session for Starter/Growth, and Enterprise SAML/OIDC, data API, expanded scale, dedicated GTM support. The same rendered page says all plans include broad engine coverage (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode/Overviews, Meta), which differs from an older Core/Enterprise matrix still indexed for the same URL. Older indexed material describes Core at $250 with 125 unique prompts and four engines, and different limits. Treat the current direct page rendering as the latest observed offer, while noting that stale/indexed surfaces exist; do not merge the two plan matrices. Confirm plan and limits at signup. [Current pricing page](https://scrunch.com/pricing/)

AXP goes beyond a checklist: first-party docs describe routing AI-user-agent requests through a customer CDN such as Cloudflare, Akamai, or Vercel to Scrunch; serving clean server-rendered HTML with no JavaScript dependency; and leaving human-facing content unchanged. The product supports uploaded or authored Markdown/HTML, deploy controls, activity logs, and version history/rollback. Some content can be re-optimized from live pages when the CMS changes; stored content requires manual refresh. Docs state Googlebot/Bingbot are not routed. This is a content delivery layer, not merely proxy-free monitoring, and implementation needs correct bot identification, CDN/DNS/routing integration, source-of-truth controls, monitoring, and a rollback plan. The vendor’s own how-to warns documentation may lag recent product updates. [How-to](https://scrunch.com/how-tos/how-to-serve-ai-optimized-content-directly-to-llms/), [AXP FAQ](https://scrunch.com/faqs/how-does-scrunch-serve-bot-friendly-html-and-pre-rendered-pages-to-ai-crawlers/), [content freshness FAQ](https://scrunch.com/faqs/how-does-scrunchs-axp-approach-compare-to-publishing-ai-friendly-content-directly-in-the-cms)

The potential value is clearest where an agent cannot extract material facts from a JavaScript-heavy site. The risk is delivering a bot-specific alternate representation: stale prices/availability or inconsistent claims can mislead agents, while the vendor says search-indexing bots are excluded but the customer still needs to validate exact routing and cache behavior. No independent controlled study was found in the reviewed sources proving AXP causes better recommendations or sales. Scrunch’s cases and homepage advertise large visibility/referral outcomes; these are customer/vendor-reported results and do not isolate AXP from other work or demonstrate profit. Monitor exact user agent, requested URL, response version, cache behavior, source freshness, and resulting citations; require approvals and fast rollback for pricing, legal, health, and product claims.

Likely profit pressures are hypotheses: enterprise sales cycles, security and CDN integration, implementation/customer-success labor, prompt collection/analysis, and ongoing infrastructure and support. Acquisition may provide Sitecore’s distribution and content platform integration, while bringing integration and product-focus execution risk. Improve with a transparent AXP technical qualification checklist, self-serve sandbox/diff/rollback, freshness policy, per-domain deployment health metrics, evidence that separates crawl access from recommendations and orders, and published onboarding/limits. A small merchant could borrow versioned, approved channel-specific rendering only if measured retrieval failures justify it; borrow the observation → diagnosis → action loop and multi-seat/multi-market controls as they scale. Avoid enterprise CDN complexity, custom implementations, unlimited-score promises, and assuming AXP pays back without a controlled outcome test. Scrunch is compelling for complex web estates; $250/month plus implementation friction is a poor fit for a small Shopify merchant when native product feeds already expose canonical product facts.

## Product opportunity for a small ecommerce merchant

Build a narrow, evidence-first catalog diagnosis product. Start with Shopify’s canonical product/variant/market facts and destination eligibility; run a small disclosed set of real buyer prompts on consumer-facing surfaces; record answer, citation, exact product/variant, market, timestamp, and repeat variation; then distinguish data defect, retrieval/access issue, and answer-selection outcome. Recommend only changes grounded in merchant source data, with approval, versioning, and rollback. Re-run the same sample after propagation and show lift as an observed association, while separating AI referral, attributed orders, and causal incrementality. Offer a free baseline plus a modest paid tier for ongoing prompts and bulk approvals; charge more only for proven catalog scale, extra markets, or service. Test willingness to pay against free native Shopify discovery and cheap SEO apps before building broad monitoring or content delivery.
