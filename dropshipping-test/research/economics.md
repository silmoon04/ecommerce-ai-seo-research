# Economics: a fast Shopify store still has to earn its way

Checked 1 October 2026. This memo models one hypothetical generic non-clothing item and one bundle. The chosen prices and landed costs below are scenarios for sensitivity analysis, not supplier quotes, market benchmarks, or evidence of demand. The existing embroidered crew figures are carried forward from the 30 September unit-economics research. No spend is authorized.

## Platform charges and scope

Shopify UK lists Basic at £25 monthly or £19/month billed yearly. Standard online cards are 2% + 25p. Shopify Payments avoids Shopify's separate third-party transaction fee; another gateway attracts Basic's 2% Shopify fee plus that provider's card charge. Do not stack it onto Shopify Payments orders. Check the actual account rate, plan, card mix and conversion costs before launch. [Shopify UK pricing](https://www.shopify.com/uk/pricing) · [Shopify fee explanation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/getting-paid-with-shopify-payments/view-payouts/pay-periods-and-fees)

Shopify Payments keeps the original card fee after a refund and charges no additional refund-processing fee. The seller may also lose product and outbound shipping costs. UK refunds can be debited outside the normal payout schedule, making the reserve a liquidity need rather than a profit expense. [Shopify refund rules](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/payouts/refunds)

Examples assume a UK-established seller, UK customers, GBP domestic cards, Shopify Payments, delivery-inclusive prices and no VAT registration. Seller location/status are unconfirmed. HMRC's £90,000 threshold applies to UK taxable turnover, while overseas sellers making taxable UK supplies may have to register without that threshold. Place of supply, imports, marketplace route and input-VAT recovery can change the result; verify the facts with HMRC or an adviser. An unregistered seller includes supplier VAT in cost and does not collect output VAT. [HMRC VAT thresholds](https://www.gov.uk/how-vat-works/vat-thresholds) · [HMRC overseas sellers](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/selling-goods-using-an-online-marketplace-or-direct-to-customers-in-the-uk)

UK distance-sale customers can generally cancel within 14 days of receipt; standard delivery is normally refunded while the card fee remains a cost. A reserve does not replace legal duties. [Consumer Contracts Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134)

## Unit model and worked examples

Let `P` be the customer's total payment, `C` supplier product plus outbound shipping, `f` the card percentage, `k` its fixed fee, `r` the full-refund share of initial orders, `q` the separate free-replacement share, `L` variable support/handling labor valued per initial order, and `A` CAC per acquired order. In this deliberately conservative model, a refunded order loses all `P`, its original processing fee and first fulfillment; replacement fulfillment is an additional `q*C`. No returned stock, reimbursement, supplier refund, chargeback, tax, or postage recovery is assumed. Expected contribution before CAC and fixed overhead is:

`M = P*(1-r) - (f*P+k) - C*(1+q) - L`.

Variable-cost break-even ROAS on initial gross receipts is `P/M` when `M > 0`; the CAC ceiling at target profit `T` per initial order is `M-T`. Target-profit ROAS is `P/(M-T)`. ROAS is revenue divided by ad spend, so these are algebraic ceilings, not performance benchmarks. Monthly shop/apps/domain and fixed setup labor still need recovery from orders; where `M-T <= 0`, the target is impossible at that price/cost combination.

Chosen generic scenario: £29.99 delivered sale; £9 landed supplier cost; 2% + £0.25 payment fee; 8% full refunds; 2% separate replacements; £0.75 valued variable labor (three minutes at £15/hour). These assumptions produce £16.81 contribution before acquisition and fixed costs, 1.78 break-even ROAS, and £11.81 maximum CAC if the desired residual is £5/order (2.54 ROAS at that CAC). At £15 CAC it retains £1.81 before fixed costs, so paid traffic at that level fails the £5 floor. Actual CAC and conversion rate are unknown. Compare acquisition spend per initial paid acquired order with this refund-adjusted initial-order contribution. Track retained orders separately; actual profit uses retained receipts and all incurred cohort costs. The calculator JSON exposes each input.

Bundle illustration: two of the same item ordinarily total £59.98 and cost £18 fulfillment. A bundle at £49.99 with the same £18 fulfillment and same refund/replacement/labor assumptions yields about £25.63 contribution per basket. The two-unit full-price same-basket control yields about £34.62. Thus this chosen 16.7% discount sacrifices approximately £8.99 contribution per basket (26.0%) before acquisition and fixed costs. It is not evidence bundles lift conversion, add units, or improve profit. A bundle wins only if the change in retained-order rate, order size, CAC, or handling cost compensates for the lost margin. Compare basket contribution per visitor or randomized eligible sessions against the same-basket control; do not celebrate average order value alone.

For comparison, the inherited Inkthreadable Changer crew hypothesis is £72 delivered, £35.31 fulfilment, 8% full refunds, 2% replacements and £2.50 variable labour. At 2% + £0.25 card processing it has £26.0338 before acquisition and fixed overhead and 2.77 variable-cost break-even gross ROAS. The prior strategy's chosen £18 contribution target permits £8.0338 CAC and needs 8.96 gross ROAS. A separate £5 sensitivity permits £21.0338 CAC and needs 3.42 ROAS. Neither floor nor the approximate £8 CAC target was user-confirmed or an industry benchmark. Keep £18 as the inherited calculator default; £5 is a sensitivity, not an authorized strategy change. No real sales, final supplier quote or willingness-to-pay evidence exists. Physical sample, identical reorder, rights and exact product facts must precede paid exposure.

## Test portfolio and cash, without double counting

Product pages can share one store, theme, domain, plan and fixed-app allowance. Charge shared costs once; assign each product its samples, fulfillment, creative and traffic. Portfolio test cost is shared setup + all product-specific costs + fixed labor, including failed/rejected offers. Report per-test results and total portfolio cash/hours.

Use the final review's replacement one-crew example: £500 capacity = £83.22 sample and identical reorder + £50 for up to two monthly Shopify cycles + £15 domain allowance + £75 total traffic + £250 protected working cash + £26.78 uncommitted. The alternative £1,000 capacity = £83.22 samples/reorder + £50 Shopify + £15 domain + £250 total traffic + £500 working cash + £101.78 uncommitted. Both use one store, Dawn, existing tools and no paid add-ons. First traffic is at most £75 in either case; a further £175 exists only inside the £1,000 alternative after a reviewed gate. These are alternative proposed capacities, not confirmed funds, spending authorization or budgets to add to the older dossier. All earlier portfolio spending reduces the remaining capacity.

The £83.22 sample arithmetic is conditional: £35.31 delivered fulfilment plus one provisional £12.60 setup charge gives £47.91 first sample; an identical reorder is £35.31. Confirm the configured quote, VAT basis and supplier terms. Two £72 refunds plus two £35.31 replacements require £214.62 before any unfunded order. A £250 working reserve leaves £35.38, so one unfunded £35.31 order leaves 7p before additional costs. Derive admission from cleared cash, actual exposed receipts, unpaid fulfilments, committed remedies and due fees/postage, increasing protection with exposed orders. Pending payouts are not cleared cash. Float and uncommitted headroom remain assets until used. All rehearsal, setup, observations, creative and test analysis share twenty founder hours; variable order labour is recorded separately.

Recommendation: use the speed of AI page assembly to reduce one offer's setup cost, then test the unresolved buyer-access question within the proposed cash and twenty-hour cap. Show £0, £5 and £18 contribution sensitivities, including fixed-cost payback and failed attempts. Organic orders are not a prerequisite for an authorized diagnostic once product quality, rights, price, delivery, returns, checkout, measurement and cash protection are ready. A £75 test can expose faults or produce early purchase evidence; calendar days cannot establish recurring demand or mature profit. Do not open a second product or add paid tools to compensate for an inconclusive result. The generic illustration cannot outrank the crew without an actual quote, reliable supply and customer evidence.
