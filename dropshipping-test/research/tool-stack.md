# Tool stack: what the “10-minute store” costs

Research checked 1 October 2026 (Europe/London). Prices are in their displayed currencies; no conversion is applied. Trials and software signals do not establish sales.

## Recommendation

For a first test, use one Shopify store, Shopify’s free Dawn theme and existing AI/chat/browser tools to build and inspect one honest product page. Keep the free Shopify Bundles app available if the offer truly needs a fixed bundle. Do not buy Atlas, Rapi and an upper TrendTrack tier together before defining a question each answers. The software can shorten page assembly and competitor browsing; it cannot supply product rights, verified claims, supplier reliability, customer service, delivery performance or demand. A published page is not a completed operating business.

The relevant paid Shopify floor is Shopify itself: its UK page lists Basic at £25/month billed monthly or £19/month when billed yearly. Dawn is free. A one-month Basic + Dawn setup is therefore £25 before payment charges, domain, fulfilment, advertising and any app. Annual Basic commits £228 upfront for the year (equivalent to £19/month) and is poor test economics if the store might close after one month. Shopify’s introductory offer currently advertises three days free followed by £1/month for three months, subject to account eligibility and offer terms. This may reduce early cash cost; £25/month is the ongoing listed monthly price. Confirm applicable tax and the actual checkout charge before budgeting. [Shopify UK pricing](https://www.shopify.com/uk/pricing) and [Dawn](https://themes.shopify.com/themes/dawn/presets/dawn), checked 1 Oct 2026.

## The named tools and their actual roles

**TrendTrack** is product/competitor intelligence, not a Shopify store builder. Its current official pricing page says monthly, quarterly (15% off) and annual (30% off) billing; it also advertises a free start. Public price responses disagree. The browser-text capture showed annual equivalents of $42/$63/$105 for Starter/Pro/Business. Direct official HTML retrieved on 1 October instead identified USD and encoded monthly prices of $49/$89/$159 with yearly monthly equivalents of $34/$62/$111. Neither is a verified checkout quote. Keep the price unresolved, confirm currency, billing period, total and tax at checkout, and do not treat an annual equivalent as one month's cash charge. The page lists API/MCP access on Starter and higher tiers, with Starter five lookups/week and two tracked brands; Pro 30/week and 10 brands; Business unlimited lookups and 20 brands. These quotas matter more than the “unlimited shops” marketing line for a small one-off scan. [TrendTrack pricing](https://www.trendtrack.io/pricing), [plan comparison](https://www.trendtrack.io/pricing), checked 1 Oct 2026.

TrendTrack’s first-party MCP documentation shows an OAuth endpoint (`https://api.trendtrack.io/v1/mcp`) and Claude/ChatGPT guides. Tools search shops, ads, brands, favorites and workspace insights; they can also write. Grants include identity/credit and discovery reads, brand-tracker and favorites read/write, and workspace-insights read. Authorize a suitable workspace, review/revoke grants as needed, and direct read-only discovery unless mutations are wanted. Claude’s plan does not include TrendTrack data; query limits apply. MCP retrieves indexed signals; it cannot validate creator sales or our conversion. TrendTrack identifies Similarweb as its traffic source. Its comparison labels EU ad reach/budget data estimated and sourced from Meta transparency reporting; that wording does not establish exact advertiser spend or audited sales. [Claude connector guide](https://docs.trendtrack.io/connect/claude), [API/MCP docs](https://docs.trendtrack.io/), [ChatGPT scopes](https://docs.trendtrack.io/en/docs/integrations/chatgpt), checked 1 Oct 2026.

**Atlas: AI Store Builder** is the current listing name at `/dropshipt` (formerly Dropshipt). Free to install for development stores, then $39/month, 7-day trial, plus additional charges as the store generates “revenue and impact”; USD billed every 30 days. No annual discount is shown. Atlas says it generates pages, advertorials, listicles, images and copy from a product source, saves templates and includes bundle/cart upsells. Its listing prohibits copying other sites’ material without rights-holder permission. Shopify lists access to customer device/activity data and merchant contact details; editing products/inventory/collections, discounts, theme and pages; reading 60 days of orders; and product-channel publication. These are declared access categories, not a privacy audit. Since it can edit a live store, use drafts and review outputs. [Atlas listing](https://apps.shopify.com/dropshipt), checked 1 Oct 2026.

**Rapid Bundles & Upsell app** is the exact current name on `/rapi`. Optional merchandising. The listing gives Free up to $100/month app-attributed revenue; Starter $15/month or $119.88/year (7-day trial, $1,000/month cap); Pro $29 or $239.88/year ($10,000 cap); Elite $59 or $479.88/year (API/MCP, no stated cap). USD; yearly amounts are prepaid, 31 to 33% below 12 monthly charges. Elite API/MCP is unnecessary for a simple offer. [Listing](https://apps.shopify.com/rapi), checked 1 Oct 2026.

**GetHookd** is used with Similarweb in the July product-research tutorial. Speedrun part one explicitly switches to TrendTrack for ad and store research; the main September build tutorial also uses TrendTrack. GetHookd’s current official site offers Starter/Pro/Team/Agency at $29/$49/$79/$129 monthly; annual billing is advertised as about 35% off, with annual Starter shown at $19/month billed yearly ($228 upfront). Every plan has a 7-day trial; credits range 50 to 800 monthly, and API/MCP is included only with annual plans. It searches ads and brands, with AI transcription/scripts/creative generation. The video’s filters (US, image ads, active, ≥25 days, top two performance tiers, max five ads/brand) narrow a research sample; they do not reveal advertiser revenue, CPA or profit. GetHookd's current score guide describes within-brand Meta impression rank, runtime and creative reuse, without access to advertiser ROAS, CTR or spend. It says the method was rebuilt, so current stars cannot retrospectively validate the July demonstration. Its estimated-spend filter combines EU reach with a chosen CPM; it is not an advertiser's actual spend export. Those are proprietary proxy signals; long-running ads can still be losing, and selection by active long-duration ads biases toward campaigns surviving long enough to be visible. Treat it as a shortlist generator, then inspect the ad, landing page, offer and rights independently. [GetHookd pricing and features](https://www.gethookd.ai/), [performance-score guide](https://gethookdai.crisp.help/en/article/understand-the-ad-performance-score-tjajzj/), [estimated-spend methodology](https://gethookdai.crisp.help/en/article/mastering-filters-1xhf011/), checked 1 Oct 2026.

## Costs and simpler alternatives

At listed ongoing monthly rates, Basic + Atlas + Rapi Starter + GetHookd Starter totals £25 + $83 per month before TrendTrack, Atlas usage charges, applicable tax/FX, payment fees, domain, products, shipping and ads. Add a verified TrendTrack quote separately. These tools are optional, and trials or introductory offers may reduce the first charge; the expression is not a complete startup budget. Annual GetHookd Starter adds $228 upfront instead of $29 monthly. Seven-day Atlas/Rapi/GetHookd trials differ from Shopify's three-day trial and subsequent promotional price. TrendTrack advertises a free start; a permanent free tier and its limits were not verified. For apps billed through Shopify, uninstalling stops future billing cycles, but already-generated charges can remain payable. Separately billed subscriptions must also be cancelled with their provider. [Shopify app billing](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/manage-billing/billing-charges/types-of-charges/third-party-charges/app-charges).

The lean alternative is Basic + Dawn, existing AI for copy and screenshots for visual research, Shopify discounts, and Shopify Bundles only for a fixed bundle. Bundles is free on all Shopify plans. Limits include 30 fixed-bundle components, 3 options/100 variants, overall (not location) inventory, and manual bundle price updates. Rapi’s mix-and-match and quantity breaks are unnecessary for one product at one price. [Shopify Bundles guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/bundles/shopify-bundles), checked 1 Oct 2026.

Claude Pro is $20 monthly or $17/month equivalent billed annually ($200 upfront). If already subscribed, drafting has no incremental subscription cost, subject to usage limits. TrendTrack remains a separate data subscription when connected to Claude. [Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing), checked 1 Oct 2026.

## Decision for our cases

For the premium anime/dark-fantasy apparel concept, Atlas and bundles do not resolve licensing, sample quality, the unvalidated £72 price, fulfilment or willingness to pay. Build one page with Dawn and licensed/original imagery. Use GetHookd only if it can answer a defined ad-pattern question; consider paying for TrendTrack only if its available free access and existing research methods cannot answer a defined competitor question. Neither makes competitor activity validated demand.

For an external merchant-facing store-builder product, buying Atlas to validate our own-store revenue is a category error: that tests one merchant’s product economics, not whether merchants pay for store-generation software. Interview/observe merchants or prototype the page workflow before paying for their SaaS subscriptions. A builder may cut layout labour from hours to minutes; the operational work remains: choose legal/available stock, set accurate price/shipping/returns, connect checkout and payments, verify tax and policies, test mobile/accessibility, place and fulfil a test order, handle cancellations/refunds, and provide support. A store that looks complete can still fail at any of those steps.

**Recommendation:** start with Basic monthly + Dawn and one page; keep all add-on apps uninstalled until a specific bottleneck is measured. If we need to compare price or page angles, define the audience, spend/time ceiling, and decision rule first. Add one tool only when its output changes a decision that we cannot make with existing tools. Keep creator screenshots and sales claims separate from vendor capability statements, and never enter competitor assets, testimonials, trust marks or revenue figures as our own.
