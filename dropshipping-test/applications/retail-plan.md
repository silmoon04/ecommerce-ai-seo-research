# Apply the method without confusing speed with a business

Research application, 1 October 2026. This is a proposed plan. The current authorization covers research only: no purchases, ads, supplier outreach, participant recruitment or customer experiments. All prices, allocations and test thresholds below are chosen planning hypotheses unless attributed to a source. UK seller, UK buyers and no VAT registration remain assumptions.

## Choose a lead and a challenger

Keep the original understated dark-fantasy embroidered crew as the lead. Its strongest potential distinction is a piece someone wants to wear because of the original artwork, restraint and garment quality. The inherited £72 delivered price is an unvalidated hypothesis. It needs evidence that the exact garment feels worth that amount, rather than a page that merely looks premium. Choose one original motif, one garment colour and the smallest workable size range after inspecting the supplier chart. Those are scope choices, not validated demand findings; do not call this a limited edition without a real limit.

Use a compact workday cable/charger organiser as the functional challenger. No exact SKU, selling price, capacity, supplier or landed cost is verified. The [product and supply memo](https://silmoon04.github.io/ecommerce-ai-seo-research/dropshipping-test/research/product-and-supply.md) favours this category for inspectable utility and lower claims burden. A proposed offer is one pouch for a clearly specified everyday device set, with photographs showing what fits. Avoid a launch bundle: the customer needs to understand the single item before we know whether multiples solve anything.

For a commodity organiser, a prettier page is insufficient distinction. The minimum credible value is documented compatibility with a useful device set, accurate dimensions, an honest packing demonstration, dependable delivery and a workable returns service at a total price the buyer accepts relative to alternatives. If we cannot provide that, the challenger should stop. This does not require a patented product, but it requires a reason to choose this seller that survives comparison. Original embroidered artwork offers another route to distinction, provided quality supports the premium.

The eventual clothing brand can build a coherent collection, identity and repeat purchases. A temporary learning store answers a narrower question about an offer and buying process. Do not attach an unrelated commodity pouch to the premium brand just to save a subscription. Prototype the crew privately and keep the organiser as a written comparison. Only the crew receives sample, page, creative and traffic allocations in this proposed round. Reuse tooling, research and accounting across the portfolio; branding should match the actual experiment.

## Adopt, change and defer the four-video method

Adopt the sequence: inspect competitor ads and pages, screen for product usefulness and fulfilment constraints, build quickly with AI, then test a specific offer. The [four-video evidence memo](https://silmoon04.github.io/ecommerce-ai-seo-research/dropshipping-test/research/video-evidence.md) reconstructs the selection tutorial, both speedruns and the main builder tutorial. Its small/light, genuine-problem and margin questions are useful screening prompts. Clothing needs additional fit, stitch, originality and identity checks. Multiple-purchase potential is optional rather than a compulsory winning-product criterion.

Change the evidence standard. Long-running ads, proprietary performance ranks and estimated visits identify hypotheses; they establish neither competitor profit nor our demand. Do not reproduce the creator's traffic-times-assumed-conversion revenue calculation. Translate a reference screenshot into a component list and shopper questions, then write original copy using verified facts. Keep price, variants, delivery, returns and seller identity easy to find. Sticky add-to-cart is optional after mobile task checks, not proof of conversion improvement.

Defer Atlas, paid intelligence subscriptions, quantity-break apps, gifts and nine-ad testing. Existing tools plus a simple page answer the first questions. Dawn is free; Shopify Basic is £25 monthly under the current UK pricing, before transaction charges and other costs ([Shopify pricing](https://www.shopify.com/uk/pricing), [Dawn](https://themes.shopify.com/themes/dawn/presets/dawn)). The [tools memo](https://silmoon04.github.io/ecommerce-ai-seo-research/dropshipping-test/research/tool-stack.md) explains why annual-equivalent prices and introductory trials do not make a month-long experiment free. Defer the day-three winner verdict and automatic 20% budget increases. Sparse orders and unreturned revenue cannot justify scale.

## One free end-to-end rehearsal

Prepare a private, non-selling crew prototype and a written organiser comparison, using existing workspace/browser tools. Spend no money and solicit no participants. Cap this stage at eight of the shared twenty founder hours: two from the four-hour evidence allocation, five from page/checkout/measurement and one from campaign/ledger analysis. This is part of the round, not extra time. Use original design assets already available or labelled conceptual placeholders. A render must not imply the uninspected embroidery or garment is an actual photograph.

Walk product page → size selection → mock cart → simulated checkout → order record → cancellation/refund record locally. Include a visibly simulated £72 delivered crew total, an unknown delivery field and an unverified supplier-cost field. Check that the selected size survives cart editing, total stays consistent, support and returns are reachable, and a refund changes retained revenue while preserving incurred costs. No card collection, public checkout or fake review is needed. Save screenshots and a ledger containing one synthetic paid order, one full refund and one replacement, all marked synthetic and excluded from real results.

Success means the journey can represent price, variant, fulfilment, refund and contribution accurately, and every unsupported public claim is identified. The outcome is operational readiness to ask the next question. It provides no willingness-to-pay evidence. Stop at eight hours even if visual polish remains; unresolved supplier facts become gate conditions, not invented copy.

## Close product and customer gaps before traffic

For the crew sample, record exact blank/model, composition, weight, colour, embroidery position and dimensions, thread finish, backing against skin and puckering. Measure chest width, length and sleeve against the chart; photograph daylight front/back, macro stitches and fit. Follow the stated care instructions for one wash and record shrinkage, distortion and colour change. One size/sample does not validate every size or durability. Check the actual UK checkout quote, embroidery/digitisation charges, fulfilment origin, stock alternatives and replacement/remorse-return treatment. Printful publishes fulfilment and shipping estimates, which must not become guaranteed arrival dates ([Printful UK shipping](https://www.printful.com/uk/shipping)).

For an organiser sample, measure usable internal/external dimensions and weight; pack the exact named charger, cables and accessories; open/close the loaded zipper repeatedly and check seams, compartments, snagging and bulging. Photograph failures too. Verify the particular supplier's stock sync, handling/transit, dispatch proof, packaging, lost-parcel process, return address and label/restocking costs. Shopify Collective describes supplier fulfilment, but its existence does not verify this SKU or connection ([Collective fulfilment](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shopify-collective/suppliers/fulfillment)).

After authorization, ask relevant prospective buyers to choose a size or determine whether their charger fits, explain the delivered cost and delivery expectation, and find cancellation instructions. Ask what they currently wear/use, their last comparable purchase and price, what makes this replace that purchase, and what information would prevent buying today. Ask for their preferred alternative and why. Record exact objections and task failures. Stated interest is weaker than actual payment; five people are a diagnostic sample, not a market estimate.

Crew creatives should test two original concepts: close-up stitch/detail followed by the full outfit, and an understated everyday styling scene explaining the original motif. Organiser concepts should demonstrate a real bag's loose contents packed into the sample, or show the exact device set fitting and being retrieved. Use original footage after sample approval, without unsupported performance claims. Published reviews require genuine evidence and permission; competitor-image regeneration supplies no automatic rights ([IPO copyright guidance](https://www.gov.uk/using-somebody-elses-intellectual-property/copyright), [ASA testimonials](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/testimonials-and-endorsements.html)).

## One proposed portfolio cash and time plan

The final [profit review](https://silmoon04.github.io/ecommerce-ai-seo-research/dropshipping-test/reviews/profit-review.md) replaces the earlier competing examples with one proposed crew route. £500 total capacity consists of £83.22 for a sample and identical reorder, £50 for up to two monthly Shopify cycles, £15 domain allowance, £75 total traffic, £250 protected working cash and £26.78 uncommitted headroom. These are planning amounts, not confirmed available funds or spending authorization. The £83.22 is conditional arithmetic: £47.91 first sample including a provisional £12.60 setup charge, then £35.31 identical reorder. Confirm the exact configured quote, VAT and repeat setup treatment. No paid apps, gifts, purchased artwork, participant incentives or second product are funded. Earlier portfolio costs consume this same ceiling; it is not an additional £500.

The alternative £1,000 example keeps the same one product, £83.22 sample/reorder and £65 shared shop/domain costs. It holds £500 working cash and £101.78 uncommitted, with £250 total traffic. The first tranche remains £75. A further £175 requires a separate review of the first result, the next learning question and cash protection. More available money does not establish that the extra test is worthwhile.

Two £72 refunds plus two £35.31 replacements require £214.62 before an unfunded order. A £250 reserve leaves £35.38, so one unpaid £35.31 fulfilment leaves 7p before added costs. Do not infer a safe concurrent order count from division by fulfilment cost. Before accepting each order, protect cleared cash for actual exposed receipts, unfunded fulfilments, committed remedies and due fees/postage; increase cover as orders accumulate. Pending payouts and unused reserve cannot fund ads. The £214.62 contingency is a starting stress case, not the maximum customer liability.

The inherited crew model gives £26.0338 before acquisition and fixed costs. Preserve its prior chosen £18 residual target and approximately £8 CAC target as planning choices. At a separate £5 sensitivity the CAC ceiling is £21.0338 and required gross ROAS 3.42; £0 residual gives £26.0338 CAC and 2.77 ROAS. These thresholds are not measured CAC or willingness to pay. Show positive contribution below £18 alongside the fixed-cost recovery needed, rather than treating it as automatic commercial failure.

One twenty-hour envelope covers four hours supplier/sample/rights/economics, five page/policies/checkout/measurement, three buyer access and observations, four sample shoot/two concepts, and four campaign QA/ledger/analysis. The eight-hour free rehearsal is included. Readiness can take longer; stop optional work and revise scope if the cap is reached. Record order support separately, fulfil accepted obligations, and include all valued labour in portfolio economics. The organiser stays on paper. A switch of route uses the remaining cash and hours, not another full allowance.

Before ads, require sample approval, rights, verified product/price/delivery/returns facts, seller details, lawful tracking configuration, tested mobile checkout and reconciled test events. Customer cancellation rights remain seller obligations; made-to-order alone is not proof of a personalised-goods exemption ([GOV.UK distance selling](https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses/distance-selling)). Paid diagnostics are allowed in this proposed route after authorization; organic orders are not a prerequisite.

Freeze one product, price, page and audience for the capped paid test. Track impressions, landing sessions, carts, checkouts, order IDs and spend; distinguish platform attribution from store records. Stop immediately for broken tracking, inaccurate claims, checkout failure, overspend or insufficient liquidity. No orders at the cap means this package failed to produce orders within that exposure, not that the product universally lacks demand.

Record actual paid amounts, fees, fulfilment, postage, discounts, refund dates, replacements, chargebacks and service minutes per order. Report provisional and mature retained orders separately, including outstanding returns. Portfolio profit subtracts all failed-test spend, shared costs and valued labour as well as order costs. Scale only after separately authorized replication, mature positive contribution after those costs, workable service/returns and sufficient working cash. A thin apparent win earns investigation; it does not earn automatic expansion.
