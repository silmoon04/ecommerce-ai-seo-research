# A capped evidence ladder instead of a three-day winner rule

Prepared 1 October 2026. This is a proposed research protocol; no customer recruitment, purchases, advertising or account changes are authorized. Amounts in GBP are chosen planning caps, not forecasts. The video's day-three example is **USD400**, with reported 3.39 revenue ROAS, and is not a GBP budget recommendation. Its day-one/day-two/day-three purchase expectations and 20% budget increases come from the speedrun's short illustrative lesson, rather than a documented sequence of profitable retained-order cohorts. [Speedrun part two](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oH2r5KUTRzo&t=1711s)

## Define the thing being tested

The unit is an offer package: exact SKU and variant, price, shipping terms, page version, creative concept, audience, channel, attribution settings and dates. A poor result cannot identify an inherently bad product when the creative or checkout failed. A good result does not establish that every audience or price will work. Keep one lead SKU and at most two creative concepts. Freeze the offer and page during a paid cohort; fixes create a new version. A platform allocating most spend to one ad has optimized delivery, not randomized a fair comparison. Use an actual split test only when the budget can support its question; otherwise describe each execution and its observed counts. [Controlled web experiments](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10618-008-0114-1)

The original embroidered crew remains the lead hypothesis because it has inspectable supply and an existing cost model. That does not establish willingness to pay the proposed GBP72. A functional organiser becomes a challenger only after a named supplier's costs and service terms are evidenced. Do not run these as simultaneous nine-ad campaigns on a tiny portfolio budget. A single coherent store can share factual seller information and infrastructure, while each product keeps its own costs, versions and outcomes.

## Resolve cheap uncertainties first

Before paid exposure, spend founder time on the exact sample, supplier quote, materials, dimensions or size chart, delivery, returns and rights. Use the four-hour supplier/sample/rights/economics allocation inside the shared twenty-hour round. If a blocking question cannot be resolved within the remaining time, stop optional activity and revise the same plan rather than adding another allowance. Sample cash needs a quote and later approval. Preserve sample photos and measurements; generated lifestyle scenes must not imply unavailable product details. Defer pain-relief products whose claims require specialist evidence. [CAP substantiation](https://www.asa.org.uk/type/non_broadcast/code_section/03.html)

A proposed first qualitative round recruits five people who plausibly buy the category, separately from friends who mainly want to encourage the founder. Five is a practical usability allowance, not statistical coverage. Ask participants to choose a variant, explain delivered price and arrival timing, find returns and complete a mock purchase task. Let them speak before showing suggested answers. Record repeated obstacles and conflicting views, without claiming a conversion rate. Use the three-hour shared buyer-access and observations allocation for recruitment, preparation, sessions and synthesis; recruit fewer than five if access takes longer. The cash plan contains no participant incentives. Recruitment and real transactions remain outside this research authorization. Fix factual or task-blocking defects before aesthetic preferences. One later round on the changed page checks whether the original obstacle remains.

Two paid creative cuts can test distinct factual reasons to consider the same item: embroidery and texture versus understated everyday styling, or organiser capacity versus a specific packing demonstration. Recut original footage before buying more production. Keep the destination and delivered offer fixed. Click response helps diagnose a hook; it does not prove buyers will pay. Do not introduce three audiences, three desires and three images together unless there is enough resource to interpret their separate outcomes.

## Verify the measurement path

Create a versioned test card and a ledger before launch. Record spend, impressions, outbound clicks, landing views, checkout starts, paid order IDs, source markers, fees, fulfilment, refunds, replacements and valued support time. Walk the mobile product-to-checkout path, including unavailable variants and changed quantities. Test event receipt and purchase value/currency; if browser and server events are both enabled, check deduplication. Reconcile one controlled order/refund path with store records using a platform test mode where available; any live transaction needs authorization. Never interpret a campaign when a broken event path explains the apparent result.

Freeze the attribution window and report platform-attributed purchases separately from ledger orders. A tracked referral or coupon is source evidence, not proof the channel caused an incremental order. Keep campaign purchase lag visible, and leave the reporting window open after spend ends rather than judging only the last day's dashboard. AI referrals, organic search, paid ads and synthetic model checks each get separate fields. A fixed prompt suite measures fact retrieval under specified conditions; it cannot measure real shopper demand. [OpenAI publisher FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12627856-publishers-and-developers-faq), [Google AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)

## Make a small paid test honest

If authorized later, use one platform, one offer, up to two concepts and a £75 total first traffic cap over a chosen seven-calendar-day window. The final £500 example has no additional traffic allocation. The alternative £1,000 example may release a further £175, bringing traffic to £250, only after a reviewed learning question, mature evidence where profit is claimed, and a cash check. Seven days is a scheduling choice, not statistical power, platform learning or profit maturity. If platform minimums cannot fit, revise or defer the test without diverting reserve. Campaign QA, ledger and interpretation use four hours inside the sole twenty-hour round. Cash and hours are portfolio totals; no SKU or application file receives another allowance.

Stop immediately for materially false claims, rights gaps, unusable checkout, wrong geography, failed events or unexpected spend. Otherwise stop at the agreed cash/date boundary. A single day without orders does not trigger an arbitrary kill, and a profitable-looking day does not release more money. Low delivery, uncertain attribution or sparse purchases may leave the result inconclusive. Preserve denominators: zero purchases among 100 independently measured relevant visits still permits a purchase rate near 3% under the approximate 95% rule of three. That illustration depends on assumptions and is not a traffic forecast. Do not convert zero tracked sales into a categorical demand verdict.

## Use mature contribution and repeat the candidate

Choose a maturity rule before observing orders: wait through the applicable delivery, cancellation/return, processing and relevant dispute exposure, label unresolved liabilities, and retain a reserve for later losses. Full chargeback maturity may take longer than a founder can wait, so publish both provisional and updated cohorts rather than calling an early dashboard final. Shopify retains original processing fees on refunded orders, which must remain in the ledger. [Shopify refund treatment](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/payouts/refunds), [UK returns guidance](https://www.gov.uk/accepting-returns-and-giving-refunds)

Compare retained revenue minus actual fees, fulfilment, refunds, replacements and valued variable labour against acquisition spend. Also show contribution per initial paid order, contribution per eligible visit where denominator quality permits, and total portfolio cash/hours including failed offers. The inherited crew scenario gives GBP26.03 before acquisition and fixed overhead once GBP2.50 labour is included; GBP28.53 excludes that labour. The inherited chosen GBP18 floor allows GBP8.0338 CAC and requires 8.96 gross ROAS. A separate GBP5 sensitivity allows GBP21.0338 CAC and requires 3.42 ROAS; the GBP0 variable-cost break-even requires 2.77 ROAS. None is an expected CAC, validated margin or user-confirmed target. Compare contribution and CAC per initial paid acquired order, with retained receipts and actual cohort costs used for realized results. Shared subscriptions count once; cash float remains a reserve until used.

An apparent profitable candidate earns a separately capped repeat cohort with the same offer and declared source, followed by a deliberate change if needed. Two small cohorts still may be uncertain; require compatible economics and reliable fulfilment before expanding. Record every rejected creative, product and cohort. Selecting the best of many attempts exaggerates its apparent performance, so the full portfolio and replication matter. [Winner's curse research](https://www.kdd.org/kdd2018/accepted-papers/view/winners-curse-bias-estimation-for-total-effects-of-features-in-online-contr)

Finally, owned-store learning can justify an internal product-fact checker. It cannot validate a merchant SaaS. That requires independent merchants with a recurring problem, a concrete workflow and actual paid continuation. The next useful evidence is a sample and a comprehensible truthful page; the next paid decision comes after the unknown budget, economic ceiling and measurement path are settled.
