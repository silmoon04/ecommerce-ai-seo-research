# Working assumptions

Research dated 30 September 2026. UK seller, UK customers and no VAT registration are modelling assumptions, not established facts about the founder. No real customer sales or willingness-to-pay evidence has been collected.

The original clothing proposal is a charcoal or moss crewneck with original small embroidery. The delivered test price is £72; an optional labelled tee is modelled at £45. These are choices to test. Supplier charges need an exact configured quote.

Refund rates, acquisition costs, time values and all experiment thresholds are scenarios or chosen operating limits. The illustrative £500 portfolio ceiling is not authorization to spend. Alternative businesses share one cash ledger; they do not each receive £500.
