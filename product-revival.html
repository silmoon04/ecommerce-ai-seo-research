The stage gates

The route to one working offer

This is the proposed route to a bounded test. These gates are not reported as passed. The recorded actuals remain zero paid orders and zero cash spend.

  1. 1

    Choose the buyer

    Define the purchase reason and the product’s useful moment.

    Gate: a specific offer and an honest claim
  2. 2

    Inspect the sample

    Verify the delivered quote, finish, use, packaging and returns.

    Gate: quality and cost evidence
  3. 3

    Make the demonstration

    Use footage and claims we have rights to publish.

    Gate: faithful product creative
  4. 4

    Screen buying interest

    Show the proposed price and truthful availability. Record useful visits and objections.

    Gate: directional interest, separate from purchases
  5. 5

    Rehearse one merchant

    Verify checkout, consent, fulfilment and refunds before one bounded order test.

    Gate: complete buying path and released funding
  6. 6

    Continue or stop

    Apply the chosen stop rule. Explain the next spend.

    Gate: evidence earns more work

Set the product, time window, effort ceiling, measurement and stop rule before publishing. An organic test still consumes samples, creator effort and owner time.

The creative test

What to film and compare

These are filming hypotheses, not finished ads or test results. Open a concept for the hook, shots, evidence needed and measurement. The real product must support every demonstration and claim.

The first batch: eight posts, one accepted sample

Try three openings with the untouched setting and one relevant alternative: six variants. Add two fresh takes of the promising cut. Test openings before settings, keep the offer fixed, and record snapshots at 72 hours and seven days. This is our workload cap. Organic differences are directional because the platform gives posts different audiences.

Read the exact sample and filming briefs. The offline register starts with six hook storyboards; it contains no produced footage or observed results.

One-order contribution scenario

Blank model. Enter every field to calculate your scenario.

Complete every field to calculate. Zero must be entered deliberately.

Formulas and what the model cannot establish

Owner time = minutes ÷ 60 × hourly value. Pre-acquisition contribution = payment − tax − landed cost − packaging − fees − loss allowance − owner time. Acquisition ceiling at the retained target = pre-acquisition contribution − desired retained contribution. Contribution after acquisition = pre-acquisition contribution − acquisition cost.

Break-even revenue ROAS = customer payment ÷ positive pre-acquisition contribution. This is a mathematical zero-contribution threshold at the entered costs, before fixed costs; it is not a prediction of attainable advertising performance. Orders to recover fixed costs = fixed costs ÷ positive contribution after acquisition, rounded up.

A negative acquisition ceiling means the desired target is already out of reach before acquisition. Income or corporation tax, setup time and unentered expenses are not accounted for. The tax field is an order-level adjustment; VAT recovery and registration require the actual treatment. These outputs are contribution scenarios, not actual profit.
