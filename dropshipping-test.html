Every editable number is a planning assumption. Generic product prices and supplier costs are unquoted. Fixed costs, taxes and other exclusions still matter.

What an initial paid order leaves

GBP per initial paid order. Full refunds retain the original fulfilment and payment fee; replacements are a separate additional fulfilment cost.

Advanced order costs & contribution target

Formula, denominator & limits

Contribution before acquisition and fixed costs = price × (1 − full refund rate) − landed fulfilment × (1 + replacement rate) − (price × payment fee rate + fixed fee) − variable labour. Contribution after acquisition subtracts CAC.

Break-even ROAS = initial price ÷ positive contribution before acquisition. ROAS at the chosen contribution target = price ÷ (contribution before acquisition − target), when that denominator is positive. Matching ROAS uses initial paid receipts before refunds and an equivalent attributable order denominator. Actual platform reporting can differ.

VAT registration and tax treatment are unconfirmed. Chargebacks, profit tax, cross-border costs, returns recovery, discounts and fixed setup labour are not included in the order model.

How many products can you actually fund?

A free shortlist of 50 candidates is different from funding 50 samples and paid traffic tests. The reserve is chosen cash held, not a forecast of liabilities.

Shared costs, reserve & valued founder time
What the cash scenario includes

Testing cash exposure = shared costs + funded products × (sample cost + traffic per product) + working reserve. Operating spend excludes the reserve. Setup time valued at an hourly rate is shown separately from cash; it is not a cash outlay unless actually paid.

The reserve is only available for the obligations that justify it. Do not treat held cash as an interchangeable advertising budget.
