Before you decide.

What print settings should I use?

A4, single-sided, actual size / 100%, with browser headers and footers off. Measure each calibration bar: its ticks must be 25 mm apart before cutting.

What supplies do I need?

Card or paper, scissors, paper glue, a ruler and a blunt scoring tool such as an empty ballpoint pen. Use supplies you already own and a protective scoring surface.

Do the buildings pack flat?

Their scored wall tubes are designed to collapse after removing the roofs. That needs a real paper test. Roofs and glued props stay rigid, so the whole kit does not store flat.

Are the preview images photographs?

No. They are original drawings based on the plans. No assembled physical sample is being claimed.

Is this a physically tested kit?

No. The complete nets have passed digital dimension, face, tab and page checks. All three buildings still need actual assembly and repeated roof-fit and fold tests.

Can I buy this here?

This is a storefront prototype with a local cart and checkout simulation. It does not accept payments, collect your address or place an order. A native Shopify product and checkout still need to be connected.

What does the proposed price mean?

It is a price to test with potential customers. Demand, support costs and any physical production costs have not yet been validated.
