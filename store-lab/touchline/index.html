Before you decide.

Why are some players given 20 minutes and others 30?

Nine outfield players share 240 minutes. Four ten-minute blocks permit six players at 30 and three at 20. Carry the actual totals forward to prioritise the less-played players next game.

Does it follow my competition's rules?

It checks this planner's capacity and minute model. Confirm match duration, squad limits and allowed substitution procedures with your competition.

Are player names uploaded?

No. The tool has no network calls, account, analytics, cookies or automatic browser storage. Use initials or shirt numbers, and keep any downloaded JSON/CSV files private.

Are there free alternatives?

Yes. SubTime's UK listing offers free use for games up to 40 minutes, with some advanced features paid. Squadd has a free administration tier, while rotation and live recording are Pro with a four-week trial. Checked 1 October 2026; compare their current terms before buying.

Can I buy this here?

This is a storefront prototype with a local cart and checkout simulation. It does not accept payments, collect your address or place an order. A native Shopify product and checkout still need to be connected.

What does the proposed price mean?

It is a price to test with potential customers. Demand, support costs and any physical production costs have not yet been validated.
