Before you decide.

Does zero count as a completed score?

Yes. Zero is entered and contributes zero points. Blank remains missing and keeps the standings provisional.

How do ties work?

Equal totals share a place. Enter a tie-break score for every team in the tied-total group; higher wins. Equal tie-break scores stay tied.

What happens if I type a bad score?

The field is highlighted and its previous valid value stays in memory. Fix it before downloading, printing or projecting results.

Can I save and continue later?

Download a JSON backup and restore it from your computer. There is no automatic saving.

Why pay when spreadsheets are free?

The proposed £8 price depends on hosts finding this offline entry, backup and projector workflow useful. That value is unvalidated, and a free Quiz Prep scoreboard is a real alternative.

Can I buy this here?

This is a storefront prototype with a local cart and checkout simulation. It does not accept payments, collect your address or place an order. A native Shopify product and checkout still need to be connected.

What does the proposed price mean?

It is a price to test with potential customers. Demand, support costs and any physical production costs have not yet been validated.
