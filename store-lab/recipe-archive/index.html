Before you decide.

Will it read the handwriting for me?

No. The prototype keeps the image beside fields for manual typing. Unclear words need checking with the source or recipe owner.

What happens after a correction?

The one proof round stays open, and any content change clears approval. Check the changed version and approve it again.

Can I use it without an account?

Yes. Open tool.html locally. No files are uploaded and no analytics run.

Is the example a real family recipe?

No. The soup and source card are fictional demonstration content. The card is an original coded illustration.

Can I buy this here?

This is a storefront prototype with a local cart and checkout simulation. It does not accept payments, collect your address or place an order. A native Shopify product and checkout still need to be connected.

What does the proposed price mean?

It is a price to test with potential customers. Demand, support costs and any physical production costs have not yet been validated.

Local preview measurements are off. If you allow them, clicks are kept in memory for this browser session. There is no analytics server. The essential cart saves product IDs and quantities on this device.
